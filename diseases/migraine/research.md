# Migraine: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [GBD 2021 Headache Collaborators, Global burden of headache disorders 1990-2021, Cell Reports Medicine 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12629824/) - about 1.2 billion people with migraine in 2021; age-standardised prevalence 14,246.6 per 100,000
- [GBD 2021 migraine burden and trends 1990-2021 (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12620191/) - 1.16 billion prevalent cases in 2021
- [WHO fact sheet: Migraine and other headache disorders](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/headache-disorders) - migraine third-highest cause of DALYs (Global Health Estimates 2021); many never diagnosed; sumatriptan unavailable in many countries; medication-overuse headache
- [ICHD-3: 1.3 Chronic migraine](https://ichd-3.org/1-migraine/1-3-chronic-migraine/) - definition: headache on 15+ days a month for 3+ months, migraine features on 8+ days
- [Natoli et al., Global prevalence of chronic migraine: a systematic review, Cephalalgia 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19614702/) - chronic migraine typically 1.4-2.2% of the population
- [Goadsby et al., A controlled trial of erenumab for episodic migraine (STRIVE), NEJM 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29171821/) - 50% responders 43.3% (70 mg) and 50.0% (140 mg) vs 26.6% placebo
- [Silberstein et al., Fremanezumab for the preventive treatment of chronic migraine (HALO CM), NEJM 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29171818/) - 50% responders 38% and 41% vs 18% placebo
- [Han et al., CGRP monoclonal antibody for chronic migraine: meta-analysis, Brain and Behavior 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6379644/) - 75% responders 12.7% vs 6.1%; 100% response rarely reported and not different from placebo
- [Dodick et al., OnabotulinumtoxinA for chronic migraine: pooled PREEMPT results, Headache 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20487038/) - Botox injections every 12 weeks reduce headache days in chronic migraine
- [Ailani et al., AHS consensus statement: integrating new migraine treatments, Headache 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34160823/) - triptans, gepants (ubrogepant, rimegepant) and ditans (lasmiditan) for attacks; preventive options
- [Charles et al., AHS position statement on CGRP-targeting therapies as first-line prevention, Headache 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38466028/) - CGRP antibodies and gepants (rimegepant, atogepant) as first-line preventives; efficacy and tolerability equal to or better than older drugs
- [Lundbeck H1 2026 interim report (19 Aug 2026)](https://www.lundbeck.com/content/dam/lundbeck-com/masters/global-site/global-site/investors/2026/q2/Q2%202026%20Company%20Announcement.pdf) - PROCEED Phase 2b positive, strongest in chronic migraine; bocunebart expected to enter Phase 3 by end of 2026
- [pharmaphorum: Lundbeck preps phase 3 for migraine prevention drug (Feb 2026)](https://pharmaphorum.com/news/lundbeck-preps-phase-3-migraine-prevention-drug) - PROCEED topline, about 430 patients with 1-4 prior preventive failures
- [PharmaShots: Lundbeck reports PROCEED Phase IIb data at AHS 2026](https://pharmashots.com/33700/lundbeck-reports-the-p-iib-proceed-trial-data-on-bocunebart-for-migraine-prevention-at-ahs-2026/) - IV arm 4.24 vs 2.86 fewer monthly migraine days vs placebo; pooled 5.94 vs 3.63
- [Pharmacy Times: AAN 2026, Lundbeck R&D chief on PACAP and bocunebart](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/aan-2026-what-is-pacap-and-why-does-it-matter-for-migraine-lundbeck-s-r-d-chief-explains-the-science-behind-bocunebart) - first new preventive mechanism since CGRP; HOPE Phase 2a in people failing 2-4 preventives
- [Pulse 2.0: TriGemX Bio raises $94M to advance Phase 3 migraine program (Oct 2026)](https://pulse2.com/trigemx-bio-raises-94-million-to-advance-phase-3-migraine-program/) - elismetrep Phase 3 underway, first results expected mid-2027
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07816367: elismetrep (K-304) for acute treatment of migraine](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07816367) - Phase 3, start 2 Oct 2026, primary completion est. Oct 2027

## Research log

- 2026-11-05: First record. Split into episodic and chronic variants; baseline sources, ratings, pipeline and forecasts.

## Open questions

- Variants: episodic and chronic were split because trial response rates and day-to-day outlook differ (about half halve attacks in episodic vs under half in chronic, from a higher starting point). The boundary is fluid: people move between the two, and the episodic rating hides a big gap between low-frequency and high-frequency episodic migraine.
- Chronic is rated 1 (borderline 2). Real-world CGRP cohorts report much better responses than trials (one reports a 100% response in about a quarter of hard-to-treat patients), but those are uncontrolled.
- Episodic burden is rated moderate assuming frequent attacks needing prevention; people with rare attacks may only need occasional triptans, which would be light.
- The bocunebart effect sizes come from trade press (PharmaShots) reporting AHS 2026 data, not a peer-reviewed paper. The `expected` date is the guided Phase 3 start, not a readout; Phase 3 results are unlikely before 2028-29.
- Lilly's PACAP antibody (LY3451838, [NCT04498910](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04498910)) reportedly showed limited efficacy in a small 38-person Phase 2 (Cephalalgia 2025; full numbers not checked), so the PACAP mechanism is not yet proven.
- No good source gives the share of people who become fully migraine-free on any treatment; trials rarely report 100% responders.
