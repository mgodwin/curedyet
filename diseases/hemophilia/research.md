# Hemophilia: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [Soucie et al., Haemophilia 2020: Occurrence rates of haemophilia among US males (CDC surveillance, PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32329553/) - incidence 1 per 4,334 live male births; 29,761-32,985 US males with hemophilia
- [CDC: Data and statistics on hemophilia](https://www.cdc.gov/hemophilia/data-research/index.html) - up to 33,000 US males; hemophilia A three to four times as common as B
- [FDA: Hemgenix approval letter (22 Nov 2022)](https://www.fda.gov/media/163466/download) - one-time AAV gene therapy for adults with hemophilia B
- [ICER final evidence report on hemophilia A and B gene therapies (Dec 2022)](https://icer.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/ICER_Hemophilia_Final_Report_12222022.pdf) - Hemgenix list price $3.5 million
- [FDA: Qfitlia (fitusiran) approval (28 Mar 2025)](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-novel-treatment-hemophilia-or-b-or-without-factor-inhibitors) - antithrombin-lowering siRNA for hemophilia A or B, dosed as rarely as every two months
- [EMA: Frehemgo (denecimig) opinion page](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/frehemgo) - positive CHMP opinion 17 Sep 2026, awaiting European Commission decision
- [Novo Nordisk: update on the denecimig BLA in the US (2 Oct 2026)](https://www.novonordisk.com/news-and-media/news-and-ir-materials/news-details.html?id=917287) - FDA review extended over facility remediation; pending EU approval
- [BioMarin: Roctavian voluntarily withdrawn from the market (23 Feb 2026)](https://www.biomarin.com/news/company-statements/biomarin-voluntarily-withdraws-roctavian-from-the-market/) - no qualified buyer found; not related to efficacy or safety
- [Mahlangu et al., NEJM 2018: Emicizumab Prophylaxis in Hemophilia A without Inhibitors (HAVEN 3, PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30157389/) - survival by subtype
- [PMLive: CSL Behring announces four-year results for haemophilia B gene therapy Hemgenix](https://pmlive.com/pharma_news/csl-behring-announces-four-year-results-for-haemophilia-b-gene-therapy-hemgenix/) - survival by subtype
