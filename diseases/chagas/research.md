# Chagas disease: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO fact sheet: Chagas disease](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/chagas-disease-%28american-trypanosomiasis%29) - 7 million infected, cure in acute phase, drugs
- [DNDi: NuestroBen trial protocol (2025)](https://dndi.org/scientific-articles/2025/new-benznidazole-regimens-chagas-disease-clinical-trial-protocol/) - Phase 3 short-course benznidazole design
- [DNDi: BENDITA shorter treatment results (2019)](https://dndi.org/press-releases/2019/study-shows-dramatically-shorter-treatment-chagas-effective-and-safer/) - 2-week course efficacy and safety
