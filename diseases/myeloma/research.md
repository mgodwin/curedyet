# Multiple myeloma: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [NCI SEER Cancer Stat Facts: Myeloma](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/mulmy.html) - ~36,000 US cases and 10,850 deaths est. 2026; lifetime risk about 0.8% (2021-2023); 5-year relative survival 63.7% (2016-2022); 202,793 living with myeloma (2023)
- [GLOBOCAN 2022 fact sheet: Multiple myeloma (IARC)](https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/globocan/factsheets/cancers/35-multiple-myeloma-fact-sheet.pdf) - 187,952 new cases and 121,388 deaths worldwide in 2022
- [Kyle et al., NEJM 2007: Clinical course and prognosis of smoldering (asymptomatic) multiple myeloma](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17582068/) - Mayo cohort of 276; progression about 10% a year for the first 5 years, cumulative 51% at 5 years, 66% at 10 and 73% at 15
- [FDA: approval of daratumumab and hyaluronidase-fihj for high-risk smoldering multiple myeloma](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-daratumumab-and-hyaluronidase-fihj-high-risk-smoldering-multiple-myeloma) - approved Nov 6, 2025, first treatment for high-risk smoldering myeloma, based on AQUILA
- [CancerNetwork: PFS, OS significantly improved by daratumumab for smoldering multiple myeloma](https://cancernetwork.com/view/pfs-os-significantly-improved-by-daratumumab-for-smoldering-multiple-myeloma) - AQUILA (NEJM 2025): HR 0.49 for progression or death; 5-year PFS 63.1% vs 40.8%; 5-year OS 93.0% vs 86.9%; grade 3-4 infections 16.1% vs 4.6%; risk criteria have since changed
- [CAR-PRISM trial listing (NCT05767359)](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05767359) - Phase 2 CAR-T in high-risk smoldering myeloma, 20 participants, active not recruiting
- [OncLive: Long-term PERSEUS data confirm durable PFS benefit with D-VRd (Sept 2026)](https://www.onclive.com/view/long-term-perseus-data-confirm-durable-pfs-benefit-with-d-vrd-in-transplant-eligible-newly-diagnosed-myeloma) - median follow-up 81.3 months; 72-month PFS 81.1% vs 56.1% (HR 0.35); 72-month OS 85.9% vs 78.6%; sustained MRD negativity 70.4% vs 36.2%; 236 stopped daratumumab after sustained MRD negativity and 175 (74%) had not restarted at a median 44 months
- [AJMC: Final CEPHEUS analysis (June 2026)](https://ajmc.com/view/in-final-cepheus-analysis-for-myeloma-median-pfs-not-reached-after-more-than-6-years) - transplant-ineligible: 72-month PFS 59.3% with DVRd vs 38.3% with VRd (HR 0.55); FDA approval of DVRd for transplant-ineligible patients Jan 2026
- [MMRF: ASCO 2026, fixed-duration therapy, MRD testing and treatment order](https://themmrf.org/mmrf-blogs/asco-2026-what-to-know-about-fixed-duration-therapy-mrd-testing-and-treatment-order/) - FORTE: sustained MRD negativity for at least 3 years gave 7-year PFS 85% and OS 97% (vs 35% and 69%); stopping treatment evidence still preliminary
- [IMF: The road to cure (Aug 2026)](https://www.myeloma.org/blog/road-cure-2026-close-finish-line) - IMS Myeloma Cure Summit, March 2026; draft cure definition: at least 5 years off therapy in complete remission, MRD negative at least 4 times about a year apart, clean imaging; some patients likely cured over two decades but a very small share
- [Lin et al., J Clin Oncol 2025: Long-term (5-year or more) remission and survival after cilta-cel in CARTITUDE-1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40459151/) - 32 of 97 patients progression-free at 5 years with no maintenance; median OS 60.7 months; authors call cilta-cel potentially curative
- [OncLive: One-third of patients with R/R myeloma progression-free at 5 years with cilta-cel](https://www.onclive.com/view/one-third-of-patients-with-r-r-myeloma-are-progression-free-at-5-years-with-cilta-cel) - serial MRD assessed in only 12 of the 32 long-term progression-free patients
- [FDA: approval of idecabtagene vicleucel for multiple myeloma](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/fda-approves-idecabtagene-vicleucel-multiple-myeloma) - first BCMA CAR-T, March 2021, after four or more prior lines; boxed warnings for cytokine release syndrome, neurotoxicity and prolonged cytopenias
- [FDA: third approval under the National Priority Voucher program (Tec-Dara)](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-grants-third-approval-under-national-priority-voucher-program) - teclistamab plus daratumumab approved Mar 5, 2026 after at least one prior line; 83% lower risk of progression or death; boxed warning for CRS and neurotoxicity; pneumonia and other infections common
- [ASCO Post: FDA approves teclistamab and daratumumab for relapsed or refractory myeloma](https://ascopost.com/issues/march-25-2026/fda-approves-teclistamab-and-daratumumab-for-relapsed-or-refractory-multiple-myeloma/) - MajesTEC-3: 587 patients; PFS HR 0.17, median 18.1 months in control arm; OS HR 0.46
- [CancerNetwork: Linvoseltamab earns FDA accelerated approval](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/linvoseltamab-earns-fda-accelerated-approval-for-r-r-multiple-myeloma) - July 2025 accelerated approval; response rate 70%, complete response 45%; boxed warning and REMS
- [Pharmacy Times: Phase 3 MonumenTAL-3 shows survival benefit with talquetamab regimens (June 2026)](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/phase-3-monumental-3-trial-demonstrates-significant-survival-benefit-with-talquetamab-based-regimens-in-relapsed-refractory-multiple-myeloma) - 864 patients; improved PFS and OS vs DPd; ICANS under 3%
- [Pharmacy Times: Elrexfio improves progression-free survival in relapsed refractory myeloma](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/elrexfio-improves-progression-free-survival-in-relapsed-refractory-multiple-myeloma) - MagnetisMM-5 met its PFS endpoint vs DPd
- [Arcellx 8-K exhibit, Feb 23, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786205/000119312526063955/d112937dex991.htm) - FDA accepted anito-cel BLA with anticipated PDUFA action date Dec 23, 2026
- [CARTITUDE-5 trial listing (NCT04923893)](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04923893) - VRd then cilta-cel vs VRd then Rd, 743 participants, primary completion est. Feb 14, 2029
- [CARTITUDE-6 trial listing (NCT05257083)](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05257083) - DVRd then cilta-cel vs DVRd then transplant, 759 participants, primary completion est. June 2033
- [MajesTEC-7 trial listing (NCT05552222)](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05552222) - Tec-DR and Tal-DR vs DRd in newly diagnosed myeloma, about 1,590 participants, primary completion est. Apr 30, 2031

## Research log

- 2026-11-05: Baseline research for the first Cure Watch record. Split into smoldering, newly diagnosed and relapsed or refractory variants, since myeloma is not staged like solid tumours and outlook and treatment differ by phase. Rated all three at level 2, with explicit discussion of the emerging "cured for some" evidence.

## Open questions

- Is any group cured for some (level 3)? Candidates: sustained MRD-negative patients after D-VRd and transplant (FORTE, PERSEUS) and the third of CARTITUDE-1 patients progression-free at 5 years off treatment. Kept at level 2 because the IMS cure definition is still a draft, PERSEUS patients who stopped daratumumab mostly remained on lenalidomide, and CARTITUDE-1 is a single Phase 1b/2 trial with limited serial MRD data. Revisit when MRD-guided stop-all-therapy data with 5-year follow-up appear, or CARTITUDE-4 long-term data confirm treatment-free remissions at earlier relapse.
- The SEER 5-year relative survival mixes all phases and is not a cure rate; most deaths in myeloma come after relapse years later.
- Smoldering myeloma share of all diagnoses and the current IMWG 2/20/20 risk model were not verified against a primary source, so no share is given.
- MagnetisMM-5 topline date and hazard ratio not confirmed from a primary source.
- AQUILA used older (Mayo 2008-style) high-risk criteria; how its benefit maps onto today's high-risk group is debated.
