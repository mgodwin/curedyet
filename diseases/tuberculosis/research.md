# Tuberculosis: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO Global TB Report 2025](https://www.who.int/teams/global-tuberculosis-programme/tb-reports/global-tuberculosis-report-2025)
- [PBS: TB cases hit record, deaths fall](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/health/global-tuberculosis-case-rise-to-a-record-but-deaths-fall-reports-who)
- [Gates MRI: M72 Phase 3 full enrollment](https://www.gatesmri.org/the-phase-3-clinical-trial-of-the-m72-aso1e-tuberculosis-tb-vaccine-candidate-has-reached-full-enrollment/)
- [EWN: BEAT-TB regimen](https://www.ewn.co.za/2026/07/01/important-advance-made-in-tb-treatment)
- [TAG CROI 2026 TB round-up](https://www.eatg.org/hiv-news/treatment-action-group-croi-2026-tb-research-round-up/)
- [M72/AS01E Phase 3 on ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06062238)
- [WHO Global Tuberculosis Report 2025: factsheet](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/global-tuberculosis-report-2025/global-tb-report-2025_factsheet.pdf) - survival by subtype
- [Review citing BCG evidence (PMC7024998)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7024998) - BCG lowers risk of meningeal and miliary TB by 85% and TB death by 66% in children
- [WHO BCG vaccines position paper, Feb 2018](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/who-wer9308-73-96) - BCG birth dose recommended; protection not consistent against all forms in all ages
- [Mangtani et al., CID 2014: BCG protection systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3905760) - BCG efficacy against lung TB varies widely by latitude and age at vaccination
- [South Africa NDoH TPT review (2022)](https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/TPT-for-ALL-Household-Contacts_PHC-Review_21June2022_v9.pdf) - Isoniazid preventive therapy reduces active TB by about 60% vs placebo (11 trials)
- [Gates MRI M72/AS01E FAQ (Jul 2026)](https://www.gatesmri.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/GatesMRI_MFR_FAQ.pdf) - Phase 3 began March 2024 in 5 countries, 20,000 enrolled; results anticipated late 2028, submissions from 2029
- [Fierce Biotech: M72/AS01E efficacy maintained to 3 years](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/gsk-tuberculosis-vaccine-maintains-efficacy-out-to-3-years) - Phase 2b about 50% protection against active lung TB in infected adults
- [CIDRAP: VPM1002 misses the mark in newborn phase 3](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/tuberculosis/novel-tb-vaccine-candidate-misses-mark-phase-3-trial-newborns) - Recombinant BCG failed non-inferiority vs BCG
