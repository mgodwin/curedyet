# ALS: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [Marin et al., Int J Epidemiol 2017: Variation in worldwide incidence of ALS, a meta-analysis (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27185810/) - pooled worldwide incidence 1.68-1.75 per 100,000 person-years
- [Chio et al., Amyotroph Lateral Scler 2009: Prognostic factors in ALS, a critical review (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19922118/) - median survival from onset 20 to 48 months
- [QurAlis: QRL-201 ANQUR trial results (24 Feb 2026)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260224163772/en/QurAlis-Demonstrates-Effects-on-Disease-Progression-and-Target-Engagement-in-ANQUR-Clinical-Trial-of-QRL-201-a-First-in-Class-Precision-Medicine-in-Development-for-Sporadic-ALS) - target engagement and pNfH reduction in Phase 1/2; pivotal Phase 3 planned for 2027
- [Ionis: FUSION phase 3 topline results](https://ir.ionis.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ionis-announces-positive-topline-results-phase-3-fusion-study)
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04856982: ATLAS, tofersen in presymptomatic SOD1 carriers](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04856982) - Phase 3, placebo-controlled; tests whether early tofersen delays clinically manifest ALS
- [FDA: approval of tofersen (Qalsody) for SOD1-ALS (Apr 2023)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/fda-approves-treatment-amyotrophic-lateral-sclerosis-associated-mutation-sod1-gene) - accelerated approval based on neurofilament reduction
