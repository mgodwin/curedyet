# Osteoporosis: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [International Osteoporosis Foundation: Epidemiology of osteoporosis and fragility fractures](https://www.osteoporosis.foundation/facts-statistics/epidemiology-of-osteoporosis-and-fragility-fractures) - 21.2% of women and 6.3% of men over 50 affected, about 500 million people; up to 37 million fragility fractures a year over 55; 1 in 3 women and 1 in 5 men over 50 will have an osteoporotic fracture; up to 20-24% die in the year after a hip fracture; 71% treatment gap in Europe in 2019
- [CDC NCHS Data Brief 405: Osteoporosis or low bone mass in older adults, 2017-2018](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db405.htm) - Age-adjusted osteoporosis prevalence 12.6% of US adults 50+ (19.6% women, 4.4% men)
- [Black et al., Lancet 1996: Alendronate in women with vertebral fractures (FIT)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8950879/) - New vertebral fractures 8.0% vs 15.0% (RR 0.53); hip fracture relative hazard 0.49
- [Cummings et al., NEJM 2009: Denosumab for fracture prevention (FREEDOM)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19671655/) - Vertebral fractures cut 68%, hip 40%, nonvertebral 20% over 36 months; injection every 6 months
- [Prolia (denosumab) US prescribing information](https://www.pi.amgen.com/united_states/prolia/prolia_pi.pdf) - Multiple vertebral fractures reported after stopping; transition to another antiresorptive; jaw osteonecrosis and atypical femoral fractures reported; boxed warning for severe hypocalcemia in advanced kidney disease
- [Neer et al., NEJM 2001: Teriparatide (PTH 1-34) in postmenopausal osteoporosis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11346808/) - Daily injections; new vertebral fractures 14% placebo vs 5% (RR 0.35); spine BMD +9 to 13 points
- [Miller et al., JAMA 2016: Abaloparatide vs placebo (ACTIVE)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27533157/) - Fewer new vertebral and nonvertebral fractures over 18 months; less hypercalcemia than teriparatide
- [Cosman et al., NEJM 2016: Romosozumab in postmenopausal women (FRAME)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27641143/) - 73% lower vertebral fracture risk at 12 months vs placebo
- [Saag et al., NEJM 2017: Romosozumab or alendronate (ARCH)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28892457/) - Romosozumab then alendronate: 48% fewer vertebral and 38% fewer hip fractures than alendronate alone in high-risk women; more serious cardiovascular events in year 1 (2.5% vs 1.9%)
- [Evenity (romosozumab) US prescribing information](https://www.pi.amgen.com/united_states/evenity/evenity_pi.pdf) - Boxed warning for heart attack, stroke and cardiovascular death; limited to 12 monthly doses
- [Eastell et al., JCEM 2019: Endocrine Society guideline on postmenopausal osteoporosis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30907953/) - Multiple drugs reduce fractures with acceptable risk-benefit; nutrition, lifestyle and fall prevention alongside drugs
- [Shoback et al., JCEM 2020: Endocrine Society guideline update on romosozumab](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32068863/) - Romosozumab an option for very high risk women, with cardiovascular caution
- [Solomon et al., JBMR 2014: Osteoporosis medication use after hip fracture in US patients 2002-2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4176766/) - Only 28.5% treated within 12 months of a hip fracture; fell from 40.2% in 2002 to 20.5% in 2011
- [FNIH: FDA qualification of first surrogate endpoint for osteoporosis trials (Dec 19, 2025)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251219872991/en/FNIH-Announces-FDA-Qualification-of-First-Surrogate-Endpoint-for-Use-in-Osteoporosis-Clinical-Trials) - Treatment-related change in total hip BMD qualified as a surrogate for fracture reduction in postmenopausal osteoporosis (SABRE, 50,000+ trial participants)
- [Entera Bio Q2 2026 results (SEC exhibit, Aug 7, 2026)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001638097/000117891326003946/exhibit_99-1.htm) - EB613 oral PTH(1-34): single ~750-woman placebo-controlled Phase 3 planned for late 2026, primary endpoint 12-month total hip BMD, topline 2H 2028; FDA accepted the plan
- [Medical Xpress: Osteoporosis fractures fall 94% in small first-in-human stem cell trial (Sep 2026)](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-09-osteoporosis-fractures-fall-small-human.html) - Moraleda et al., Cell 2026: one infusion of HCELL-edited autologous MSCs in 10 women; no treatment-related adverse events; fractures 8 to 0.5 a year over two years
- [Fierce Biotech: Nanox nets FDA clearance for osteoporosis and spine fracture AI (2022)](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/nanox-nets-fda-clearance-osteoporosis-and-spine-fracture-ai) - HealthOST AI flags vertebral compression fractures and low bone density on spine CT scans taken for any reason

## Open questions

- Women affected worldwide: the often-quoted "200 million women" is an older IOF figure; the current IOF page gives 21.2% of women over 50 and about 500 million people in total. The record uses the current page.
- Level 2 rather than 3: anabolic drugs can raise density out of the osteoporosis range for some people, but no source shows a lasting return to normal fracture risk without further treatment, so "cured for some" is not established.
- The June 2026 date for FDA agreement on EB613's Phase 3 comes from search summaries of an Entera filing; the August 2026 release confirms the agreement but not the exact month.
- Teriparatide's US two-year limit was removed from the label in 2020, though courses are still usually about two years; abaloparatide is limited to two years and romosozumab to 12 months.
- No recent US figure on treatment after hip fracture newer than 2011 was checked; the gap may have changed.
