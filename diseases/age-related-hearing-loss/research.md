# Age-related hearing loss: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO fact sheet: Deafness and hearing loss (March 2026)](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/deafness-and-hearing-loss) - about 430M with disabling hearing loss, more than 25% of people over 60, nearly 80% in low- and middle-income countries, unaddressed loss costs almost US$1 trillion a year
- [NIDCD: Quick statistics about hearing](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/statistics/quick-statistics-hearing) - disabling loss in about 22% of US adults 65-74 and 55% of 75+; about 30% of adults 70+ who could benefit have ever used hearing aids; cochlear implant counts
- [NIDCD: Cochlear implants](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/cochlear-implants) - implants need surgery and significant therapy to learn to hear with them; adults who lost most hearing later in life can benefit
- [FDA: OTC hearing aids, what you should know](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/hearing-aids/otc-hearing-aids-what-you-should-know) - OTC category in effect October 17, 2022, for adults 18+ with perceived mild to moderate loss
- [UPI: FDA finalizes rule to let Americans buy hearing aids without prescription (Aug 2022)](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/US/2022/08/16/FDA-finalizes-rule-to-let-Americans-buy-hearing-aids-without-prescription/6351660657809/) - final rule Aug 16, 2022; administration estimated savings of about $2,800 per pair, implying prescription pairs cost thousands
- [Lin et al., ACHIEVE randomized trial (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10737284/) - 977 adults 70-84 with untreated mild to moderate loss; hearing intervention did not reduce 3-year cognitive decline overall; a preplanned analysis in the higher-risk ARIC cohort showed 48% less decline
- [Livingston et al., Dementia prevention, intervention, and care: 2024 Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) - hearing loss estimated at about 7% of dementia population attributable fraction (a modelled, population-level figure that assumes causation)
- [Business Wire: Frequency Therapeutics FX-322 Phase 2b topline (Feb 2023)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230213005212/en) - FX-322-208 missed its speech-perception primary endpoint; program discontinued
- [Pharmacy Times: FDA grants accelerated approval to Otarmeni (Apr 23, 2026)](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-to-otarmeni-first-and-only-gene-therapy-for-genetic-hearing-loss) - first hearing gene therapy, for biallelic OTOF variants; 11 of 12 children improved in CHORD
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05788536: DB-OTO CHORD study](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05788536) - Regeneron OTOF gene therapy trial
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07032038: First-in-human trial of Rincell-1 with a cochlear implant](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07032038) - Phase 1/2a, 20 participants, primary completion April 2027
- [Pioneer Group: Rinri gets green light for first-in-human Rincell-1 trial](https://thepioneergroup.com/rinri-therapeutics-gets-the-green-light-to-launch-first-in-human-trial-for-rincell-1/?amp=1) - MHRA approval July 2025; 10 of 20 participants with presbycusis
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07637981: DX243 Phase 1/2a](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07637981) - about 24 adults 18-75 with mild to moderate loss, enrolling from May 2026, primary completion October 2027
- [Brussels Times: Belgian biotech drug for age-related hearing loss (2026)](https://www.brusselstimes.com/2169801/potential-drug-developed-by-a-belgian-biotech-can-reverse-age-related-hearing-loss) - first dose of DX243 given; aims at the biology of presbycusis

## Research log

- 2026-11-05: New record. Rated level 2 (controlled) with moderate burden; forecasts, pipeline and toll ratio added. Framed as the common age-related loss, not congenital or genetic deafness.

## Open questions

- **Level 2, not 1, is a judgement call.** The ladder's level 1 is "eases symptoms but it still advances", and presbycusis does keep advancing under hearing aids. We chose level 2 ("near-normal life on ongoing treatment") because aids, and implants for severe loss, restore enough everyday communication for most users that it resembles control on lifelong treatment, the way glasses do for presbyopia. Against that: aids restore audibility more than clarity, understanding speech in noise often stays hard, and only about 30% of older adults who could benefit have ever used aids. A reviewer could reasonably put it at 1.
- **Why not "Cured for most" with devices.** A hearing aid or implant is a prosthesis that has to be worn for life; take it off and the loss is still there, so it is not a cure.
- **Burden moderate.** Daily lifelong device use plus meaningful cost (prescription pairs in the thousands of US dollars, partly eased by OTC aids since 2022); cochlear implants are surgery plus months of rehabilitation, which alone would be heavy, but they are only for the severe minority.
- **Dementia evidence is stated narrowly.** ACHIEVE missed its primary endpoint; the 48% reduction is from a preplanned subgroup and is hypothesis-generating. The Lancet Commission's ~7% figure is a population attributable fraction that assumes a causal link.
- **Gene therapy is a signal only.** Otarmeni treats a rare inherited single-gene form in an otherwise healthy cochlea; nothing comparable exists for presbycusis, which involves hair cells, synapses, nerve cells and the stria vascularis at once.
- **DX243 details** (mechanism, exact trial phase label) come from the registry and one news report; the company's own release was not found. Its primary completion date (Oct 2027) is an estimate.
- **Rincell-1 start** is not confirmed by a first-patient-dosed release; the April 2027 completion is the registry estimate and may slip.
- Forecast starts from the base rate for a level-2 condition with no Phase 3 curative candidate (normal-pace chance by 2035 under 5%).
