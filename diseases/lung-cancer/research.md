## Research log

- 2026-11-15: Pulled into this update out of turn by an FDA rejection. Summit announced on Nov 15 a complete response letter for ivonescimab plus chemotherapy in EGFR-mutated NSCLC after TKI: the FDA said progression-free benefit without a significant overall survival benefit does not establish effectiveness. The check on approval by year end resolved no. The drug targeted late-stage control, not cure, so level and forecast are unchanged. Next milestone set to the taletrectinib sNDA decision (Jan 4, 2027).
- 2026-11-04: Added status.groups rating each subtype on the cure ladder with its own burden and survival.
