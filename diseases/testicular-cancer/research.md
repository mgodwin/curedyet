# Testicular cancer: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [American Cancer Society: Testicular cancer survival rates](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/testicular-cancer/detection-diagnosis-staging/survival-rates.html) - 5-year survival by stage
- [Cancer Research UK: Testicular cancer survival statistics](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/health-professional/cancer-statistics/statistics-by-cancer-type/testicular-cancer/survival) - 10-year survival 96.5%, up from 62.5% in the 1970s
- [How Einhorn helped turn a deadly cancer into a curable disease (OncLive, 2014)](https://www.onclive.com/view/how-einhorn-helped-turn-a-deadly-cancer-into-a-curable-disease) - 1974 cisplatin study; cure rates before and after
- [American Cancer Society: Key statistics for testicular cancer (2026)](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/testicular-cancer/about/key-statistics.html) - 2026 US cases and deaths
