# Bladder cancer: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [GLOBOCAN 2024 bladder fact sheet (IARC)](https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/globocan/factsheets/cancers/30-bladder-fact-sheet.pdf) - 635,264 cases (8th) and 227,626 deaths (13th) worldwide in 2024
- [NCI SEER Cancer Stat Facts: Bladder cancer](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/urinb.html) - 84,530 US cases and 17,870 deaths est. 2026; 2.1% lifetime risk (2021-2023); 5-year relative survival 79.1% overall, 98.0% in situ (50% of cases), 73.0% localized (34%), 41.8% regional (7%), 9.6% distant (6%), 2016-2022
- [NCI PDQ: Bladder cancer treatment (health professional)](https://www.cancer.gov/types/bladder/hp/bladder-treatment-pdq) - 70-80% of new bladder cancers are non-muscle-invasive; 80% recurrence risk over 20+ years after resection of Ta/T1 tumors; 39% of TaG3 vs 5% of TaG1 progressed over 15-20 years; muscle-invasive disease can sometimes be cured
- [NCI: Bladder cancer causes and risk factors](https://www.cancer.gov/types/bladder/causes-risk-factors) - tobacco a major risk factor; workplace chemicals, arsenic in well water, schistosomiasis (common in Africa and the Middle East)
- [Cancer Research UK: Bladder cancer survival](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/bladder-cancer/survival) - 5-year survival in England, diagnosed 2016-2020: stage 1 more than 75%, stage 2 50%, stage 3 more than 40%, stage 4 more than 10%
- [BCAN: BCG shortage](https://bcan.org/bcg-shortage-bladder-cancer/) - Merck the only US supplier of TICE BCG since 2012; shortage updates since February 2019; new plant expected by late 2026 to triple capacity; reduced-dose BCG and mitomycin as substitutes
- [FDA approves durvalumab with BCG for high-risk NMIBC (May 28, 2026)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-durvalumab-combination-bacillus-calmette-guerin-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder) - POTOMAC, 1,018 patients; disease-free survival HR 0.68
- [Targeted Oncology: FDA approves intravesical gemcitabine system (Sept 9, 2025)](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/fda-approves-intravesical-gemcitabine-delivery-for-bladder-cancer) - Inlexzo (TAR-200) for BCG-unresponsive CIS; 82% complete response in SunRISe-1 cohort 2, about half lasting 1 year; device stays 3 weeks per cycle
- [J&J medical information: SunRISe-3](https://jnjmedicalconnect.com/products/tar-200/medical-content/tar-200-sunrise-3-study) - Phase 3 of TAR-200 with or without cetrelimab vs BCG in BCG-naive high-risk NMIBC; results not yet published
- [FDA: Adstiladrin (nadofaragene firadenovec) approval (Dec 16, 2022)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-disco-burst-edition-fda-approval-adstiladrin-nadofaragene-firadenovec-vncg-patients-high-risk) - first gene therapy for BCG-unresponsive CIS; 51% complete response; median response 9.7 months; every three months
- [Urology Times: BOND-003 24-month results (Dec 2025)](https://www.urologytimes.com/view/bond-003-cretostimogene-yields-durable-24-month-responses-in-high-grade-nmibc) - cretostimogene 75.5% anytime complete response, 41.8% at 24 months, no grade 3+ treatment-related events
- [Nasdaq: CG Oncology BLA progress (June 2026)](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/cg-oncology-touts-cretostimogene-data-bla-progress-bladder-cancer-push) - rolling BLA begun Q4 2025; clinical modules complete; CMC module and completion guided for Q4 2026
- [FDA approves durvalumab for muscle-invasive bladder cancer (Mar 28, 2025)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-durvalumab-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer) - NIAGARA, 1,063 patients; EFS HR 0.68, OS HR 0.75
- [FDA approves pembrolizumab with enfortumab vedotin for MIBC (Nov 21, 2025)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-pembrolizumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer) - KEYNOTE-905/EV-303, 344 cisplatin-ineligible patients; EFS HR 0.40, OS HR 0.50 vs surgery alone; warnings for skin reactions, high blood sugar, lung inflammation
- [Pharmacy Times: FDA extends EV-pembrolizumab to all cystectomy candidates (July 10, 2026)](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/pembrolizumab-plus-ev-regimens-receive-fda-approval-for-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer) - KEYNOTE-B15/EV-304, 808 cisplatin-eligible patients; EFS HR 0.53; OS HR 0.65; 24-month OS 86.9% vs 81.3%; pathologic complete response 55.8% vs 32.5%
- [ASCO Post: KEYNOTE-B15 at ASCO GU 2026](https://ascopost.com/news/march-2026/neoadjuvantadjuvant-doublet-significantly-improved-survival-in-patients-with-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer/) - 24-month EFS 79.4% vs 66.2%
- [Zlotta et al., Lancet Oncol 2023 via ASCO Post: trimodality therapy vs cystectomy](https://ascopost.com/news/may-2023/radical-cystectomy-vs-trimodality-therapy-for-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer/) - 722 patients; 5-year metastasis-free survival 74-75% in both; salvage cystectomy in 13%
- [FDA approves enfortumab vedotin with pembrolizumab for advanced urothelial cancer (Dec 15, 2023)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-pembrolizumab-locally-advanced-or-metastatic-urothelial-cancer) - EV-302, 886 patients; median OS 31.5 vs 16.1 months
- [CancerNetwork: EV-302 long-term follow-up (May 2026)](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/enfortumab-vedotin-pembrolizumab-shows-long-term-survival-benefit-in-1l-urothelial-carcinoma) - median follow-up 42.8 months; median OS 33.6 vs 15.9 months; 42-month OS 44.0% vs 24.6%
- [LabMedica: AI pathology test gets FDA Breakthrough designation (May 2026)](https://mobile.labmedica.com/pathology/articles/294810629/ai-pathology-test-receives-fda-breakthrough-for-bladder-cancer-risk-stratification.html) - Valar Labs' Vesta risk test from routine slides in NMIBC

## Open questions

- Non-muscle-invasive level 4 is a judgement: deaths are uncommon but recurrence is the rule (about 80% over 20 years in one series). A long-term bladder-cancer-specific survival figure by risk group (low, intermediate, high) would help. Burden is rated heavy for the typical patient because of repeated resections, years of cystoscopy and BCG for high-risk cancers; low-risk cancers alone would be moderate.
- Muscle-invasive survival data predate perioperative enfortumab vedotin plus pembrolizumab (Nov 2025, July 2026) and durvalumab (Mar 2025). If 5-year KEYNOTE-B15 results hold near 2-year levels, the variant could approach level 4 for fit patients, but many people with this stage are older or frail.
- BCG supply: Merck's new plant was expected to open by late 2026; whether it has and when allocation ends is unconfirmed. A patient forum claim that it opened in March 2025 could not be verified.
- Cretostimogene's application completion is company guidance (Q4 2026); a PDUFA date would follow FDA acceptance, likely in 2027.
- The disease-level 79% 5-year figure is pulled up by the half of cases found as non-invasive tumors; the record treats the invasive stages as the real bottleneck.
