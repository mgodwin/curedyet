# Hepatitis C: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO hepatitis C fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hepatitis-c)
- [Egypt and the 2026 WHO report (EMPHNET)](https://epinews.emphnet.net/en/news/health-minister-who-2026-report-cements-egypts-global-leadership-in-eliminating-hepatitis-c)
- [AI-powered HCV test-and-treat trial](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07793643)
- [Page et al., NEJM 2021: vaccine regimen to prevent chronic HCV (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8367093) - ChAd3/MVA vaccine safe and immunogenic but did not prevent chronic HCV in 548 people who inject drugs
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07237282: phase 1 adjuvanted protein HCV vaccine](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07237282) - AVIHepC1 phase 1, not yet recruiting as of Sep 2026
- [European Vaccine Initiative: World Hepatitis Day 2026 (HCVIVA)](https://www.euvaccine.eu/post/world-hepatitis-day-2026-hepatitis-let-s-break-it-down) - HCVIVA inactivated vaccine still moving toward first-in-human trials
- [Platt et al., Cochrane 2017: NSP and OST for preventing HCV](https://cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.CD012021.pub2/information) - Opioid substitution therapy cuts HCV acquisition about 50%; low-quality evidence
