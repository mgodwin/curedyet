# Plague: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO plague fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/plague) - bubonic fatality 30-60% untreated; pneumonic and septicemic always fatal untreated; pneumonic can kill in 18-24 hours, high recovery if treated within 24 hours; most 2019-2025 cases in DRC and Madagascar; 7-day prophylaxis for contacts; vaccination only for high-risk groups
- [Swissinfo: WHO seeks details on possible second death in Irkutsk (Oct 2026)](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/international-geneva/who-reports-of-a-possible-second-death-in-irkutsk/92184836) - WHO: just under 4,000 cases and 423 deaths from 10 countries, 2019-2025; Irkutsk plague institute death
- [CDC: Plague maps and statistics](https://www.cdc.gov/plague/maps-statistics/index.html) - about 5 US cases a year (range 0-17), rural West (NM, AZ, CO; CA, OR, NV), over 80% bubonic
- [Kugeler et al., Epidemiology of human plague in the United States, 1900-2012, Emerg Infect Dis 2015](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/21/1/14-0564) - fatality 66% pre-antibiotic vs 16% after 1942; bubonic 13%, septicemic 27% (21/78), pneumonic 36% (5/14) in antibiotic era; 9% died with effective antibiotics vs 52% without; ~13% since 1965
- [Nelson et al., Antimicrobial treatment and prophylaxis of plague, CDC MMWR Recomm Rep 2021;70(3)](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/70/rr/rr7003a1.htm) - first-line drugs by form; 10-14 days treatment; 7-day post-exposure prophylaxis; primate doxycycline 88% survival at 6 h vs 75% at 15 h; no natural resistance reported in the US; previous vaccines lacked efficacy against pneumonic plague
- [Randremanana et al., A trial of ciprofloxacin vs aminoglycoside-ciprofloxacin for bubonic plague (IMASOY), NEJM 2025 (PMC author manuscript)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7618094) - 450 enrolled, 222 infected; failure 9.0% vs 8.1%; 5 vs 4 deaths; 3 days in hospital then daily village health worker visits
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04110340: IMASOY](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04110340) - trial registration
- [WHO Disease Outbreak News: Plague, Madagascar (27 Nov 2017)](https://www.who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/27-november-2017-plague-madagascar-en) - 2,348 cases, 202 deaths (8.6%), 1,791 pneumonic (most suspected or probable); 7,289 contacts given prophylaxis, 11 became suspected cases; 33 isolates all antibiotic-sensitive
- [Vallès et al., Human plague: an old scourge that needs new answers, PLoS NTD 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7451524) - 2013-2018: 2,886 cases, 504 deaths (17.5%), 95% sub-Saharan Africa; country counts (Madagascar 2,323, DRC 410, Peru 40, US 40); plasmid-mediated resistance in three Madagascar strains; streptomycin hard to obtain; EV76 live vaccine abandoned, EV-NIIEG still used in Russia and parts of Asia; rF1V and SV1 passed Phase 2, no Phase 3
- [Guiyoule et al., Transferable plasmid-mediated resistance to streptomycin in a clinical isolate of Yersinia pestis, Emerg Infect Dis 2001](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2631670) - streptomycin-resistant Madagascar strain
- [Institut Pasteur: Researchers sound the alarm (2007)](https://www.pasteur.fr/en/researchers-sound-alarm) - 1995 multidrug-resistant Madagascar strain carried a plasmid shared with Salmonella
- [Sun et al., Developing live vaccines against plague, J Infect Dev Ctries 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3932668) - Plague Vaccine USP not available in the US since 1999; no controlled efficacy trials
- [Hart et al., Advanced development of the rF1V and rBV A/B vaccines, Adv Prev Med 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3199075) - killed whole-cell Plague Vaccine USP production discontinued 1999 and not effective against pneumonic plague; rF1V biodefense development history
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07207408: rF1V-1018 vaccine Phase 2 (Dynavax)](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07207408) - start 11 Sept 2025, 148 adults, active not recruiting, primary completion est. 29 Jan 2027
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05506969: rF1V with CpG 1018 vs rF1V Phase 2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05506969) - 200 adults, completed April 2024, results posted July 2025
- [ISRCTN79243381: PlaVac Uganda, ChAdOx1 Plague Phase 1b](https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN79243381) - Oxford viral-vector vaccine in healthy Ugandan adults
- [Precision Vaccinations: Oxford launches plague vaccine study (2021)](https://precisionvaccinations.com/2021/07/28/oxford-launches-plague-vaccine-study) - UK Phase 1 of ChAdOx1 Plague
- [Healio: FDA approves levofloxacin for plague (Apr 2012)](https://cm.healio.com/news/ooi/20120427/fda-approves-new-plague-treatment) - Animal Rule approval for treatment and prophylaxis; 94% of treated monkeys survived vs none on placebo
- [NBC News: Arizona patient dies in emergency room from plague (Jul 2025)](https://www.nbcnews.com/health/health-news/arizona-patient-dies-plague-rcna218251) - pneumonic plague death, Coconino County, first there since 2007
- [The Star: Madagascar reports 4 confirmed plague cases (14 Oct 2026)](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2026/10/14/madagascar-reports-4-confirmed-plague-cases) - start of the 2026-27 season

## Open questions

- The 2019-2025 WHO totals come from WHO officials' remarks reported in news (Oct 2026); the underlying WHO publication (likely a Weekly Epidemiological Record review) was not found. One secondary summary gave 3,847 cases; replace with the WHO document when located.
- The brief and older coverage say about 7 US cases a year; CDC's current statistics page says about 5. The record uses CDC's current figure.
- Treated fatality figures for septicemic and pneumonic plague rest on very small US series (78 and 14 cases over 70 years) and include late diagnoses.
- Whether WHO or CDC guidelines will adopt oral ciprofloxacin monotherapy for bubonic plague after IMASOY, and when.
- Russia's live EV-NIIEG vaccine is licensed and used there, but there is little published efficacy data in people; it is not counted as strong protection.
- PlaVac Uganda results were not located; its registry status should be rechecked.
