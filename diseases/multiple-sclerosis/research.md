# Multiple sclerosis: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [Atlas of MS 2024 update, Multiple Sclerosis Journal 2026: Global prevalence of multiple sclerosis surpasses 3 million (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42693747/) - 3.1 million people living with MS worldwide in 2024
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06451159: KYV-101 CD19 CAR-T in treatment-refractory progressive MS](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06451159) - Phase 1, open-label; no efficacy results posted
- [Genentech: Fenebrutinib FDA filing acceptance](https://www.gene.com/media/press-releases/15130/2026-09-29/genentechs-fenebrutinib-is-the-first-btk)
- [Sanofi: Cenrifki (tolebrutinib) approved in the EU for nrSPMS (23 Jun 2026)](https://www.sanofi.com/en/media-room/press-releases/2026/2026-06-23-05-00-00-3315699) - EU approval for secondary progressive MS without relapses
- [Sanofi: update on tolebrutinib US submission in nrSPMS (24 Dec 2025)](https://www.sanofi.com/en/media-room/press-releases/2025/2025-12-24-06-00-00-3210238) - FDA issued a complete response letter
- [European Medicines Agency: Ocrevus (ocrelizumab) EPAR overview](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/ocrevus) - survival by subtype
