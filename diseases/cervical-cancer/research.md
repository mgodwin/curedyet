# Cervical cancer: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO fact sheet: Cervical cancer (3 Jul 2026)](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/cervical-cancer) - about 604,000 cases and 280,000 deaths in 2024; 90-70-90 targets; elimination threshold of 4 per 100,000 women; HPV testing every 5-10 years from 30; women with HIV 6 times more likely to develop it
- [WHO Cervical Cancer Elimination Initiative](https://www.who.int/initiatives/cervical-cancer-elimination-initiative) - nearly 94% of cervical cancer deaths in 2022 were in low- and middle-income countries
- [GLOBOCAN 2024 cervix uteri fact sheet (IARC)](https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/globocan/factsheets/cancers/23-cervix-uteri-fact-sheet.pdf) - 604,196 cases and 279,583 deaths in 2024
- [Global cancer statistics 2024 (CA Cancer J Clin 2026)](https://doi.org/10.3322/caac.70090) - leading cause of cancer death in women in 26 countries, mostly in sub-Saharan Africa; mortality 8.3 vs 4.3 per 100,000 in transitioning vs transitioned countries; about 31% of girls had a first HPV dose in 2024; 91 of 194 countries used single-dose schedules by March 2026
- [NCI SEER Cancer Stat Facts: Cervical cancer](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/cervix.html) - 13,490 US cases and 4,200 deaths est. 2026; 0.6% lifetime risk; stage at diagnosis 41% localized, 37% regional, 16% distant; 5-year relative survival 68.8% overall, 91.8% localized, 64.0% regional, 20.5% distant
- [Cancer Research UK: Cervical cancer survival](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/cervical-cancer/survival) - 5-year net survival by stage, England 2013-2017: stage 1 about 95%, stage 2 almost 70%, stage 3 more than 40%, stage 4 about 15%
- [Swift et al., J Low Genit Tract Dis 2020: recurrence after treatment for CIN3 and AIS](https://doi.org/10.1097/LGT.0000000000000542) - 15,177 treated women; 5-year recurrence 6.1% for CIN3 and 9.0% for AIS; treatments LEEP, laser, cone, hysterectomy
- [Long-term oncologic and obstetric outcomes after LEEP (BMC Women's Health 2024)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10863218/) - 13.5% treatment failure for CIN2-3; LEEP as standard outpatient treatment; obstetric risks after excision; raised CIN and cancer risk for up to 25 years
- [FDA approves pembrolizumab with chemoradiotherapy for FIGO 2014 stage III-IVA cervical cancer (Jan 2024)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-pembrolizumab-chemoradiotherapy-figo-2014-stage-iii-iva-cervical-cancer) - KEYNOTE-A18 approval and dosing
- [OncLive: final KEYNOTE-A18 data (Jun 2025)](https://www.onclive.com/view/final-phase-3-data-underscore-role-of-pembrolizumab-chemoradiotherapy-in-high-risk-locally-advanced-cervical-cancer) - OS HR 0.73; 36-month OS 81.8% vs 74.4%
- [McCormack et al., Lancet 2024: INTERLACE](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01438-7) - induction carboplatin/paclitaxel before chemoradiation; 5-year OS 80% vs 72% (HR 0.60)
- [ASCO Post: SHAPE trial, simple vs radical hysterectomy (Plante et al., NEJM 2024)](https://ascopost.com/issues/may-25-2024/noninferiority-of-simple-vs-radical-hysterectomy-for-pelvic-recurrence-in-low-risk-cervical-cancer) - 3-year pelvic recurrence 2.52% vs 2.17%; less urinary incontinence and retention
- [Plante et al., NEJM 2024: Simple versus radical hysterectomy in women with low-risk cervical cancer](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2308900) - primary SHAPE paper
- [FDA approves tisotumab vedotin for recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer (Apr 2024)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-tisotumab-vedotin-tftv-recurrent-or-metastatic-cervical-cancer) - full approval on innovaTV 301; median OS 11.5 vs 9.5 months
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06079671: eVOLVE-Cervical](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06079671) - Phase 3 volrustomig after chemoradiation, FIGO 2018 stage IIIA-IVA, 800 participants, primary completion est. 26 Nov 2027
- [Edaily: Celltrion CT-P73 wins FDA fast track (Aug 2026)](https://en.edaily.co.kr/news/eda202608135135/) - tissue factor ADC for recurrent or metastatic disease after platinum chemotherapy; in Phase 1
- [Lei et al., NEJM 2020: HPV vaccination and the risk of invasive cervical cancer](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1917338) - Sweden; 88% lower risk when vaccinated before 17
- [JNCI commentary on Scottish linkage study (Palmer et al. 2024)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11160490/) - no invasive cancers among women vaccinated at 12-13
- [ASCO Post: HPV vaccine protection sustained long term (Wu et al., BMJ 2026)](https://ascopost.com/news/march-2026/hpv-vaccination-protection-from-cervical-cancer-sustained-long-term/) - 926,362 Swedish women; IRR 0.21 vaccinated before 17; no waning over 18 years
- [WHO: one-dose HPV vaccine offers solid protection (Apr 2022)](https://www.who.int/news/item/11-04-2022-one-dose-human-papillomavirus-(hpv)-vaccine-offers-solid-protection-against-cervical-cancer) - single-dose schedule
- [WHO fact sheet: Immunization coverage](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/immunization-coverage) - 33% of girls had a first HPV dose in 2025; 162 countries had HPV vaccine in national programmes
- [PubMed: Quadrivalent HPV vaccine, the first vaccine for cervical cancers](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17998730/) - FDA approved Gardasil on 8 June 2006
- [Australian Immunisation Handbook: HPV](https://immunisationhandbook.health.gov.au/contents/vaccine-preventable-diseases/human-papillomavirus-hpv) - national school programme for girls 12-13 began April 2007
- [Australian Government: National Strategy for the Elimination of Cervical Cancer](https://www.health.gov.au/resources/publications/national-strategy-for-the-elimination-of-cervical-cancer-in-australia) - aims for elimination (under 4 per 100,000) by 2035
- [MedTech Dive: FDA clears Hologic AI cervical cytology system (2024)](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/hologic-fda-clearance-ai-cervical-cancer-screening/706509/) - AI-assisted digital Pap test review

## Open questions

- Stage survival from England (2013-2017) predates the 2018 FIGO staging change (positive lymph nodes now stage III) and the 2024 additions of pembrolizumab and induction chemotherapy; newer stage-specific data may move stage II toward 85%.
- Stage 0 reached and reached_easy years are approximate; no single source dates when CIN3 treatment became standard or when LEEP became the usual treatment.
- Prevention: if HPV vaccination plus screening brings rich countries below WHO's elimination threshold (Australia targets 2035), the methodology's "reliably prevented at population scale" wording could argue for a higher cure-ladder rating; for now cure is rated on treatment.
- Global gap: GLOBOCAN gives only a twofold average mortality gap between transitioning and transitioned countries, but country-level gaps are far larger; a sourced country comparison would sharpen the toll text.
