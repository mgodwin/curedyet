# Head and neck cancer: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [GLOBOCAN 2024 lip and oral cavity fact sheet (IARC)](https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/globocan/factsheets/cancers/1-lip-oral-cavity-fact-sheet.pdf) - 452,205 cases and 194,108 deaths in 2024
- [GLOBOCAN 2024 oropharynx fact sheet (IARC)](https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/globocan/factsheets/cancers/3-oropharynx-fact-sheet.pdf) - 107,887 cases and 51,823 deaths in 2024
- [GLOBOCAN 2024 hypopharynx fact sheet (IARC)](https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/globocan/factsheets/cancers/5-hypopharynx-fact-sheet.pdf) - 91,464 cases and 49,748 deaths in 2024
- [GLOBOCAN 2024 larynx fact sheet (IARC)](https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/globocan/factsheets/cancers/14-larynx-fact-sheet.pdf) - 193,937 cases and 96,619 deaths in 2024; the four sites sum to about 845,000 cases and 392,000 deaths (nasopharynx and salivary glands excluded)
- [NCI SEER Cancer Stat Facts: Oral cavity and pharynx cancer](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/oralcav.html) - 60,480 US cases and 13,150 deaths est. 2026; 1.2% lifetime risk; 5-year relative survival 69.9% overall, 88.7% localized (26% of cases), 69.7% regional (55%), 36.0% distant (12%), 2016-2022
- [NCI SEER Cancer Stat Facts: Laryngeal cancer](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/laryn.html) - 12,290 US cases and 3,960 deaths est. 2026; 0.3% lifetime risk; 5-year relative survival 62.5% overall, 79.8% localized (49%), 49.4% regional (30%), 36.0% distant (15%), 2016-2022
- [NCI: Head and neck cancers fact sheet](https://www.cancer.gov/types/head-and-neck/head-neck-fact-sheet) - tobacco and alcohol cause most mouth and voice box cancers; about three-quarters of oropharyngeal cancers caused by HPV; more than twice as common in men; Gardasil 9 accelerated approval June 2020 for preventing HPV-related oropharyngeal and other head and neck cancers, ages 9-45; radiation causes swallowing difficulty and dry mouth
- [WHO fact sheet: Oral health](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/oral-health) - tobacco, alcohol and areca nut are leading causes of oral cancer; burden tracks socioeconomic circumstances; HPV a growing share in young people in North America and Europe
- [Gillison et al., Lancet 2019: RTOG 1016 (PubMed)](https://www.pubmed.gov/30449625/) - 849 HPV-positive oropharyngeal patients; 5-year OS 84.6% with cisplatin vs 77.9% with cetuximab; 5-year PFS 78.4% vs 67.3%; similar toxicity
- [Nguyen-Tan et al., JCO 2014: RTOG 0129 long-term report](https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2014.55.3925) - 8-year OS 70.9% vs 30.2% for p16-positive vs p16-negative oropharyngeal cancer
- [ENTtoday: RTOG 0129 HPV analysis (Ang et al., NEJM 2010)](https://enttoday.org/?p=8575) - 5-year survival above 75% for HPV-positive vs under 50% for HPV-negative after chemoradiation
- [Chen, Curr Oncol Rep 2025: De-escalation for HPV-positive oropharyngeal cancer](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11976804/) - RTOG 1016, De-ESCALaTE (2-year OS 98% vs 89% favouring cisplatin), NRG-HN002, ECOG 3311 and PATHOS (about 1,100 patients, results not reported); swallowing outcomes after reduced dose
- [ASCO Post: NRG-HN005 (Apr 2025)](https://ascopost.com/news/april-2025/study-finds-standard-chemoradiation-therapy-superior-to-deintensification-approaches-for-hpv-related-oropharyngeal-cancer) - 382 patients; 2-year PFS 98.1% standard vs 88.6% (60 Gy + cisplatin) and 90.3% (60 Gy + nivolumab); neither met non-inferiority
- [ASCO Post: FDA approves perioperative pembrolizumab (June 2025)](https://ascopost.com/news/june-2025/fda-approves-neoadjuvant-and-adjuvant-pembrolizumab-for-resectable-locally-advanced-head-and-neck-cancer) - approved June 12, 2025, PD-L1 CPS 1 or more; KEYNOTE-689, 714 patients; median EFS 59.7 vs 29.6 months (HR 0.70); first new approval in six years
- [CancerNetwork: Perioperative pembrolizumab in locally advanced HNSCC (KEYNOTE-689, NEJM 2025)](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/perioperative-pembrolizumab-enhances-survival-in-locally-advanced-hnscc) - 3-year OS 68.2% vs 59.2% in CPS 10 or more (HR 0.72); overall OS not yet significant at first interim; eligible: oral cavity, larynx, hypopharynx and p16-negative oropharynx stage III-IVA, and p16-positive oropharynx only if T4
- [ASCO Post: NIVOPOSTOP (Aug 2025)](https://ascopost.com/issues/august-25-2025/adding-nivolumab-to-adjuvant-chemoradiotherapy-reduces-risk-of-recurrence-in-patients-with-head-and-neck-cancer/) - 680 patients; 3-year DFS 63.1% vs 52.5% (HR 0.76); grade 4 events 13.1% vs 5.6%; Lancet publication Dec 22, 2025; OS pending
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03576417: NIVOPOSTOP](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03576417) - active, not recruiting; primary completion 2027-08
- [Harrington et al., Eur J Cancer 2025: KEYNOTE-048 5-year follow-up (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40262400/) - 5-year OS 14.4% pembrolizumab vs 6.5% EXTREME; 16.0% pembrolizumab-chemotherapy vs 5.2%
- [CancerNetwork: Petosemtamab plus pembrolizumab improves OS (Oct 29, 2026)](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/petosemtamab-pembrolizumab-improves-os-in-first-line-pd-l1-positive-hnscc) - LiGeR-HN1 met OS and response endpoints at first interim; about 50% lower risk of death regardless of HPV status; Genmab to engage regulators
- [Chaturvedi et al., JCO 2018: HPV vaccination and oral HPV infection (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29182497/) - NHANES; about 88% lower prevalence of oral HPV 16/18/6/11 in vaccinated young adults; population effect modest because of low uptake, especially in men
- [CIDRAP: HPV vaccination and head and neck cancer (2026)](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/human-papillomavirus-hpv/people-vaccinated-against-hpv-have-lower-risk-head-and-neck-cancer-study) - Int J Infect Dis; 1.4 million records; vaccination at 9-26 linked to 68% fewer head and neck and 91% fewer oropharyngeal cancers; small case numbers, observational
- [Nikolov et al., JMIR 2021: Deep learning segmentation of head and neck anatomy](https://www.jmir.org/2021/7/e26151/) - 3D U-Net outlines 21 organs at risk on planning CT at expert level

## Research log

- 2026-11-05: First full record. Split into HPV-positive oropharyngeal cancer (non-metastatic), early and advanced HPV-negative cancer, and recurrent or metastatic disease, because HPV status changes outlook more than site does. Survival from SEER (2016-2022), RTOG 1016 and KEYNOTE-048. Prevention block placed on the HPV-positive variant only, since HPV vaccination does not prevent tobacco- and alcohol-related cancers.

## Open questions

- HPV-positive level 4 rests mainly on trial survival (RTOG 1016, 84.6% at 5 years). A population-based 5-year figure for p16-positive oropharyngeal cancer in the US or England would settle whether it is truly above 85%.
- Early HPV-negative level 4 is a judgement: SEER localized figures mix HPV-positive and HPV-negative and different sites, and voice box cancer alone is 80%. Site-specific stage I-II data (England or SEER by AJCC stage) would help.
- SEER and most trial survival data predate perioperative pembrolizumab (June 2025) and petosemtamab; 5-year outcomes will take years.
- Prevention level 2 is a judgement call: protection against oral HPV infection is strong, but protection against oropharyngeal cancer itself rests on small observational studies. FDA's 2020 approval was accelerated, based on cervical and anogenital efficacy.
- Toll excludes nasopharyngeal (about 120,000 cases/yr) and salivary gland cancers, which are biologically different; the SEER oral cavity and pharynx figures include nasopharynx.
- KEYNOTE-689 included p16-positive oropharyngeal cancers only if T4; the pipeline item is tagged to the advanced HPV-negative variant only, since that was nearly all of the trial.
