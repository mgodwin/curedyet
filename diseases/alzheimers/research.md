## Research log

- 2026-11-22: Pulled into the batch for the AR1001 Phase 3 readout at CTAD (Nov 16-17). The monotherapy population (n=604) met the primary endpoint (CDR-SB -0.44, P=.027); the overall population (n=1,535) did not (P=.40). A small effect from a symptomatic drug does not move the cure forecast, which is unchanged.
- 2026-11-15: Full review. No approval, rejection or pivotal readout yet this month. AriBio presents POLARIS-AD (AR1001) topline results at CTAD on Nov 16; Roche will show trontinemab imaging data and the PrevenTRON design. Forecast and the AR1001 check (15%) unchanged pending the readout.
- 2026-11-03: Baseline research for the first Cure Watch snapshot (level, treatment, pipeline, forecasts).

## Open questions

- AR1001 POLARIS-AD reads out Nov 16, 2026; resolve chk-alzheimers and revisit the pipeline next update.
- An April 2026 Cochrane review judged anti-amyloid antibody benefits not clinically meaningful; drugmakers dispute it. Level 1 already reflects modest benefit.
- AR1001 filing plans after FDA discussions are not yet announced.
