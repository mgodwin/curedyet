# Spinal muscular atrophy: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [Kolb et al., Annals of Neurology 2017: Natural history of infantile-onset SMA (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29149772/) - leading genetic cause of infant death; incidence about 1 in 11,000 live births
- [Verhaart et al., Orphanet J Rare Dis 2017: Prevalence, incidence and carrier frequency of 5q-linked SMA (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28676062/) - incidence around 1 in 10,000 live births
- [MedlinePlus Genetics: Spinal muscular atrophy](https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/condition/spinal-muscular-atrophy/) - affects 1 per 8,000 to 10,000 people worldwide
- [HealthDay: FDA approves Itvisma](https://www.healthday.com/healthpro-news/neurology/fda-approves-itvisma-for-spinal-muscular-atrophy)
- [EC authorisation of Itvisma](https://assets.sma-europe.eu/EC_market_authorisation_Itvisma_July_2026_f8bbf80e37.pdf)
- [FDA: FDA approves first therapy to target muscle loss in SMA (Sep 2026)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/fda-approves-first-therapy-target-muscle-loss-spinal-muscular-atrophy) - Isembyld (apitegromab-mstn) for patients 2 and older already on SMN2-targeted treatment
- [Scholar Rock: FDA approval of Isembyld (11 Sep 2026, SEC 8-K exhibit 99.1)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1727196/000110465926107273/tm2625362d1_ex99-1.htm) - company announcement of US approval
- [EMA: Isembyld withdrawal letter (Aug 2026)](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/withdrawal-letter/withdrawal-letter-isembyld_en.pdf) - original EU application withdrawn over GMP issues at the Catalent Indiana fill-finish site
- [Scholar Rock: apitegromab MAA resubmitted to EMA (Oct 2026)](https://investors.scholarrock.com/news-releases/news-release-details/scholar-rock-resubmits-apitegromab-marketing-authorisation/) - resubmission with an alternate fill-finish site
- [Strauss et al., Nature Medicine 2022: Onasemnogene abeparvovec for presymptomatic infants with three copies of SMN2 (SPR1NT, PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35715567/) - survival by subtype
- [Day et al., Lancet Neurology 2021: Onasemnogene abeparvovec for symptomatic infantile-onset SMA (STR1VE, PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33743238/) - survival by subtype
