# Celiac disease: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [Global prevalence of celiac disease: systematic review and meta-analysis (2018)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29551598/) - 1.4% by serology, 0.7% by biopsy
- [Beyond Celiac: drug development pipeline](https://www.beyondceliac.org/research/drugdevelopment/drug-development-pipeline) - no approved drugs; pipeline list
- [HCPLive: TEV-'408 meets primary endpoint in celiac trial (2026)](https://www.hcplive.com/view/tev-408-meets-primary-endpoint-in-celiac-trial) - Phase 2a result
