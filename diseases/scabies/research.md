# Scabies: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO fact sheet: Scabies](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/scabies) - 200 million affected, recommended treatment
- [NICE evidence review: ivermectin versus permethrin](https://www.nice.org.uk/advice/esuom29/chapter/evidence-review-efficacy) - comparative efficacy
- [ClinicalTrials.gov: moxidectin in adults with scabies](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05875441) - Phase 2 design
