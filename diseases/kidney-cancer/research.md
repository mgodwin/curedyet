# Kidney cancer: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [NCI SEER Cancer Stat Facts: Kidney and renal pelvis cancer](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/kidrp.html) - 80,450 US cases and 15,160 deaths est. 2026; 1.8% lifetime risk (2021-2023); stage at diagnosis 66% localized, 17% regional, 15% distant; 5-year relative survival 79.2% overall, 93.6% localized, 77.6% regional, 20.3% distant (2016-2022); incidence rising 0.7%/yr, deaths falling 1.1%/yr
- [GLOBOCAN 2024 kidney fact sheet (IARC)](https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/globocan/factsheets/cancers/29-kidney-fact-sheet.pdf) - 442,570 new cases and 144,871 deaths worldwide in 2024
- [ACS: What is kidney cancer?](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/kidney-cancer/about/what-is-kidney-cancer.html) - about 9 in 10 kidney cancers are renal cell carcinoma; about 70% of those are clear cell
- [ACS: Key statistics for kidney cancer](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/kidney-cancer/about/key-statistics.html) - average age 65; twice as common in men; rising diagnoses partly from incidental findings on CT scans
- [ACS: Treatment of kidney cancer by stage](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/kidney-cancer/treating/by-stage.html) - partial or radical nephrectomy; active surveillance for some small tumors; cryoablation, radiofrequency ablation and SBRT; adjuvant pembrolizumab with or without belzutifan; vena cava surgery in stage III; immunotherapy and targeted drugs for stage IV
- [NCI PDQ: Renal cell cancer treatment (patient)](https://www.cancer.gov/types/kidney/patient/kidney-treatment-pdq) - stage definitions (stage I 7 cm or smaller in kidney; stage II larger than 7 cm in kidney; stage III veins, perinephric fat or nearby nodes; stage IV beyond Gerota's fascia, adrenal or distant); first-line nivolumab plus ipilimumab, pembrolizumab plus axitinib, pembrolizumab plus lenvatinib, nivolumab plus cabozantinib
- [Cancer Research UK: Kidney cancer survival](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/kidney-cancer/survival) - 5-year net survival by stage, England 2016-2020: stage 1 almost 90%, stage 2 around 75%, stage 3 around 75%, stage 4 around 15%
- [UroToday: AUA 2021 renal mass guideline update](https://www.urotoday.com/conference-highlights/siu-2021/133893-siu-2021-aua-lecture-2021-renal-mass-guideline-update.html) - active surveillance should be discussed with all patients with localized renal masses; thermal ablation an alternative for solid masses under 3 cm
- [Finelli et al., J Clin Oncol 2017: ASCO guideline on small renal masses](https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2016.69.9645) - active surveillance, ablation and partial nephrectomy for small renal masses
- [ASCO Post: KEYNOTE-564 overall survival (May 2024)](https://ascopost.com/issues/may-25-2024/keynote-564-adjuvant-pembrolizumab-improves-overall-survival-vs-placebo-in-renal-cell-carcinoma) - OS HR 0.62; 48-month OS 91.2% vs 86.0%; supported the November 2021 approval; up to 17 cycles (about a year)
- [FDA approves belzutifan with pembrolizumab for adjuvant treatment of RCC (Jun 12, 2026)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-belzutifan-pembrolizumab-adjuvant-treatment-renal-cell-carcinoma) - LITESPARK-022, 1,841 patients; DFS HR 0.72; OS immature; belzutifan up to 54 weeks
- [News-Medical: LITESPARK-022 at ASCO GU 2026](https://www.news-medical.net/news/20260301/A-new-standard-for-preventing-post-surgery-kidney-cancer-relapse.aspx) - about 81% vs 74% disease-free at median 28.4 months
- [ASCO Post: FDA approval of adjuvant belzutifan with pembrolizumab (Jun 2026)](https://ascopost.com/news/june-2026/fda-approval-in-renal-cell-carcinoma-adjuvant-belzutifan-with-pembrolizumab) - approval summary; belzutifan warnings include anemia and hypoxia
- [FDA approves belzutifan for advanced RCC (Dec 2023)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-belzutifan-advanced-renal-cell-carcinoma) - LITESPARK-005 after PD-1/PD-L1 and VEGF-TKI; PFS HR 0.75
- [Targeted Oncology: FDA approves belzutifan plus lenvatinib (Sep 2026)](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/fda-approves-belzutifan-lenvatinib-advanced-ccrcc) - LITESPARK-011 PFS 14.6 vs 10.6 months (HR 0.74); OS HR 0.85, not significant
- [CancerNetwork: LITESPARK-012 misses primary end points (Apr 2026)](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/litespark-012-trial-does-not-meet-primary-end-point-for-advanced-rcc-group) - adding belzutifan or quavonlimab to pembrolizumab plus lenvatinib did not improve PFS or OS
- [UroToday: CheckMate 214 8-year follow-up (Ann Oncol 2024)](https://urotoday.com/recent-abstracts/urologic-oncology/renal-cancer/153947-nivolumab-plus-ipilimumab-versus-sunitinib-for-first-line-treatment-of-advanced-renal-cell-carcinoma-extended-8-year-follow-up-results-of-efficacy-and-safety-from-the-phase-iii-checkmate-214-trial.html) - OS HR 0.72 (ITT); 90-month PFS 22.8% vs 10.8%; complete response 12.0% vs 3.5%
- [Exelixis Q1 2026 results (SEC 8-K exhibit)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000939767/000093976726000058/exel20260331exhibit991.htm) - STELLAR-304 topline expected second half of 2026; LITESPARK-033 and -034 with Merck
- [UroToday: PEAK-1 trial design at ASCO 2026](https://www.urotoday.com/conference-highlights/asco-2026/asco-2026-kidney-cancer/169374-asco-2026-peak-1-a-randomized-double-blind-active-control-multicenter-phase-3-trial-of-casdatifan-and-cabozantinib-versus-placebo-and-cabozantinib-in-patients-with-advanced-clear-cell-rcc.html) - casdatifan plus cabozantinib vs placebo plus cabozantinib; about 720 patients
- [HealthManagement.org: deep learning for identifying benign small renal masses on CT](https://healthmanagement.org/c/imaging/News/deep-learning-for-identifying-benign-small-renal-masses-on-ct-scans) - Radiology study of 1,703 patients; model comparable to radiologists; about 14-20% of resected small masses are benign

## Research log

- 2026-11-05: First full record. Split into AJCC stages I-IV with England stage survival from Cancer Research UK (2016-2020) and US stage shares from SEER. Covered nephrectomy, surveillance and ablation, adjuvant pembrolizumab and belzutifan, and first-line immunotherapy combinations.

## Open questions

- SEER figures cover kidney and renal pelvis cancers together; renal pelvis cancers are urothelial and behave differently, but SEER does not split stage survival by type.
- Stage survival from England (2016-2020) mostly predates adjuvant pembrolizumab (Nov 2021) and fully predates adjuvant belzutifan (Jun 2026); stage II and III may already be closer to 85%. The England stage 2 figure (around 75%) also looks low next to US localized survival (94%), perhaps because of older patients and other causes of death.
- Stage IV is rated 1 although about 1 in 10 on first-line immunotherapy combinations have lasting complete responses; if long-term data show these are cures, it could arguably be rated 3 like melanoma stage IV.
- STELLAR-304 topline was guided for the second half of 2026 and had not been found reported as of 2026-11-05; the expected date is set to end of 2026.
- Stage I reached year (1970) is approximate; no single source dates it.
