# COVID-19: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO: Global health gains face threat of reversal (World health statistics 2026, May 2026)](https://www.who.int/news/item/13-05-2026-global-health-gains-face-threat-of-reversal) - 22.1 million excess deaths 2020-2023, more than three times the 7.0 million reported COVID-19 deaths
- [WHO: World health statistics 2026](https://www.who.int/publications/b/82976) - excess deaths 22.1 million (95% UI 20.6-23.6 million) vs 7.0 million reported; two-thirds of excess deaths in people 65+
- [WHO COVID-19 Global Risk Assessment, version 10 (Aug 2026)](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/covid-19-global-risk-assessment--version-10) - global risk moderate; weekly reported deaths below 500 in 2026; 6,914 deaths from 27 countries through 28 June 2026 (about 266 a week); 88% of deaths in people 65+; thin reporting
- [CDC MMWR: Early detection and surveillance of SARS-CoV-2 variant BA.3.2 (Mar 2026)](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/75/wr/mm7510a1.htm) - estimated 390,000-550,000 hospitalizations and 45,000-64,000 deaths in the 2024-25 US season; BA.3.2 evades antibodies in lab studies
- [WHO fact sheet: Post COVID-19 condition (long COVID), Feb 2025](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/post-covid-19-condition-(long-covid)) - definition (start within 3 months, last at least 2 months); about 6 in 100 people with COVID-19 develop it; common symptoms; limited treatment evidence, symptom care and rehabilitation; vaccination appears to reduce the risk
- [Al-Aly et al., Nature Medicine 2024: Long COVID science, research and policy](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39122965/) - around 400 million people cumulatively affected worldwide; about $1 trillion a year economic impact
- [CDC NCHS Data Brief 480: Long COVID in adults, United States, 2022](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db480.htm) - 6.9% of adults ever had long COVID, 3.4% had it at interview (NHIS 2022)
- [Hammond et al., NEJM 2022: Oral nirmatrelvir for high-risk, nonhospitalized adults with Covid-19 (EPIC-HR)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2118542) - 89% fewer hospitalizations or deaths in unvaccinated high-risk adults treated within 3 days
- [Healio: Trials demonstrate Paxlovid's evolving role against COVID-19 (Jun 2026)](https://www.healio.com/news/infectious-disease/20260622/trials-demonstrate-paxlovids-evolving-role-against-covid19) - PANORAMIC (3,516) and CanTreatCOVID (716), NEJM 2026: no significant reduction in hospitalization or death in mostly vaccinated adults; median recovery 8 vs 12 days in PANORAMIC; Lane and Fauci editorial
- [Pharmacy Times: Nirmatrelvir-ritonavir speeds recovery but fails to reduce hospitalizations in vaccinated high-risk adults (May 2026)](https://pharmacytimes.com/view/nirmatrelvir-ritonavir-recovery-but-fails-to-reduce-hospitalizations-in-vaccinated-high-risk-adults) - timing of the PANORAMIC/CanTreatCOVID publication
- [Gottlieb et al., NEJM 2022: Early remdesivir to prevent progression to severe Covid-19 in outpatients (PINETREE)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2116846) - three days of IV remdesivir cut hospitalization or death by 87% in unvaccinated high-risk outpatients
- [Jayk Bernal et al., NEJM 2022: Molnupiravir for oral treatment of Covid-19 in nonhospitalized patients (MOVe-OUT)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2116044) - about 30% relative reduction in hospitalization or death in unvaccinated adults
- [Butler et al., Lancet 2023: Molnupiravir plus usual care vs usual care (PANORAMIC)](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(22)02597-1/fulltext) - no reduction in hospitalization or death in vaccinated high-risk adults; faster recovery
- [RECOVERY Collaborative Group, NEJM 2021: Dexamethasone in hospitalized patients with Covid-19](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2021436) - deaths cut by about a third in ventilated patients and a fifth in those on oxygen; no benefit without oxygen
- [WHO: Therapeutics and COVID-19, living guideline (Aug 2025)](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/B09540) - corticosteroids, IL-6 receptor blockers (tocilizumab) and baricitinib for severe and critical COVID-19; antivirals for non-severe disease at high risk
- [Wiegand et al., JAMA Network Open 2026 (via Pharmacy Times): 2025-26 COVID-19 vaccine effectiveness](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/updated-2025-2026-covid-19-vaccine-nearly-halved-ed-visits-hospitalizations-in-cdc-funded-study) - VE 55% against hospitalization (53% in 65+) and 50% against ED/urgent care, Sept-Dec 2025, median 6-7 weeks after the dose; CDC declined to publish in MMWR; European data suggest waning after 2 months
- [CDC MMWR: Interim estimates of 2024-25 COVID-19 vaccine effectiveness (Feb 2025)](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/74/wr/mm7406a1.htm) - 45-46% against hospitalization in immunocompetent adults 65+ in the first 7-119 days
- [Managed Healthcare Executive: Updated COVID-19 vaccine cut hospitalization risk by 35% in 2024-25 season (Jun 2026)](https://www.managedhealthcareexecutive.com/view/updated-covid-19-vaccine-cut-hospitalization-risk-by-35-in-2024-2025-season) - over a full season (7-299 days) VE 35% against hospitalization, 41% against critical illness, lower in immunocompromised; waning across groups
- [AARP: FDA approves newest COVID vaccines (Sep 2026)](https://www.aarp.org/health/conditions-treatments/fda-approves-newest-covid-vaccines/) - four 2026-27 XFG vaccines approved for adults 65+ and younger people with a high-risk condition
- [FDA: COVID-19 vaccines (2026-2027 formula) for use in the United States beginning in fall 2026](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/industry-biologics/covid-19-vaccines-2026-2027-formula-use-united-states-beginning-fall-2026) - monovalent JN.1-lineage XFG formula, VRBPAC 28 May 2026
- [HealthDay: FDA approves updated COVID vaccines, but fewer people will be eligible (Aug 2025)](https://www.healthday.com/health-news/coronavirus/fda-approves-updated-covid-vaccines-but-fewer-people-will-be-eligible) - 2025 approvals first limited to 65+ and younger people with risk conditions
- [Invivyd: Twelve months advance notice of EUA termination for Pemgarda (Jul 2026)](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/invivyd-announces-receipt-of-twelve-months-advanced-notice-of-emergency-use-authorization-eua-termination-for-pemgarda-and-provides-an-update-on-next-steps-with-the-u-s-fda) - EUA ends 29 June 2027; no FDA BLA guidance yet; pre-exposure prophylaxis for people 12+ with moderate to severe immune compromise; anaphylaxis boxed warning

- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06631287: REVERSE-LC, baricitinib for long COVID](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06631287) - Phase 3, 550 planned, recruiting; primary completion listed 1 Nov 2026 (estimated), completion 1 July 2027

## Research log

- 2026-11-09: No new approvals, rejections or pivotal readouts found this month. Forecasts unchanged.
- 2026-11-05: Baseline research for the first Cure Watch record. Split into acute COVID-19 and long COVID because their treatment, outlook and burden differ sharply. Disease level anchored on acute COVID-19 (1, as for flu). Prevention rated 1 at disease level and on both variants.

## Open questions

- Acute level 1 follows the flu record: antivirals clearly cut hospital stays in unvaccinated people at high risk (EPIC-HR, PINETREE), but the 2026 PANORAMIC/CanTreatCOVID trials found no significant effect in mostly vaccinated adults. A case could be made for 2 for people at high risk.
- Prevention rated 1 rather than 2: early-season VE against hospitalization (45-55%) sits right at the 50% line, but full-season VE (about 35%) is below it, and VE against infection or ED visits is lower. In 2020-21 the first mRNA vaccines briefly reached about 90%+ against symptomatic disease before variants and waning; that is not counted as reaching rung 3 because it did not last.
- The 2025-26 VE figure is early-season (median 6-7 weeks after the dose) and only about 12% of hospitalized patients had the updated dose.
- Pemivibart's 84% protection against symptomatic COVID-19 comes from a secondary source and was not verified against FDA documents, so it is not used in the record.
- Current global yearly deaths are not reliably known: WHO now receives death reports from only about 27 countries. The US 2024-25 CDC estimate is used as the best current yearly figure for a rich country; a 2025-26 CDC death estimate was not found.
- REVERSE-LC's registry primary completion (Nov 2026) looks unlikely given about half enrollment in July 2026; the July 2027 completion date is used.
- Long COVID level 1 assumes symptom treatments ease but do not change its course. Long COVID prevalence estimates vary widely by definition (US surveys range roughly 3-8% of adults depending on year and wording). No 2023-25 NHIS figure was found.
- Long COVID prevention: WHO says vaccination appears to reduce the risk, but no reliable single effect size was found, so the rung rests on that and on the single COVID-OUT metformin result.
