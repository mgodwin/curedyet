# Thyroid cancer: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [American Cancer Society: Thyroid cancer survival rates](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/thyroid-cancer/detection-diagnosis-staging/survival-rates.html) - 5-year survival by type and stage
- [American Cancer Society: What is thyroid cancer?](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/thyroid-cancer/about/what-is-thyroid-cancer.html) - share of each type
- [SEER Cancer Statistics Review 1975-2012: Thyroid survival by diagnosis year](https://seer.cancer.gov/archive/csr/1975_2012/results_single/sect_26_table.08.pdf) - 92% 5-year survival for 1975-77 diagnoses
- [Seidlin, Marinelli and Oshry 1946, JAMA (History of Medicine)](https://historyofmedicine.com/id/11520) - first radioactive iodine treatment of metastatic thyroid cancer
- [American Cancer Society: Key statistics for thyroid cancer (2026)](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/thyroid-cancer/about/key-statistics.html) - 2026 US cases and deaths
