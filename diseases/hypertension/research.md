# High blood pressure: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO fact sheet: Hypertension (25 Sep 2025)](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hypertension) - 1.4 billion adults aged 30-79 (33%) in 2024; 44% unaware; 44% diagnosed and treated; 320 million (23%) controlled
- [WHO: Uncontrolled high blood pressure puts over a billion people at risk (23 Sep 2025)](https://www.who.int/news/item/23-09-2025-uncontrolled-high-blood-pressure-puts-over-a-billion-people-at-risk) - second global hypertension report; more than 10 million deaths a year; two-thirds of cases in low- and middle-income countries
- [CDC: High blood pressure facts (updated Jun 2026)](https://www.cdc.gov/high-blood-pressure/data-research/facts-stats/) - nearly half of US adults (48.1%) have high blood pressure; about 1 in 4 controlled; 680,179 US deaths with high blood pressure as primary or contributing cause in 2024
- [ACC: Ten points to remember, 2017 ACC/AHA high blood pressure guideline](https://www.acc.org/Latest-in-Cardiology/Ten-Points-to-Remember/2017/11/09/11/41/2017-Guideline-for-High-Blood-Pressure-in-Adults) - first-line drug classes; screening for secondary causes; a specific cause is found in about 10% of adults with hypertension
- [2017 ACC/AHA guideline for high blood pressure in adults (Hypertension)](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/HYP.0000000000000065) - full guideline, including the section on secondary hypertension
- [AHA scientific statement on resistant hypertension (2018)](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/HYP.0000000000000084) - resistant hypertension in about 12-15% of treated patients; spironolactone as preferred add-on; screen for secondary causes
- [Ettehad et al., Lancet 2016: Blood pressure lowering for prevention of cardiovascular disease (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26724178/) - each 10 mmHg systolic reduction cuts major cardiovascular events by about 20%
- [TCTMD: FDA approves baxdrostat (May 2026)](https://www.tctmd.com/news/fda-approves-baxdrostat-inadequately-controlled-hypertension) - first aldosterone synthase inhibitor; BaxHTN placebo-corrected systolic drop of 8.7-9.8 mmHg
- [Mineralys Q1 2026 results: lorundrostat NDA accepted](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/1752148/mineralys-therapeutics-reports-first-quarter-2026-financial-results-and-provides-corporate-update) - PDUFA date Dec 22, 2026
- [Genentech/Alnylam: zilebesiran advances to Phase 3 ZENITH (Aug 2025)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250830170505/en/Genentech-and-Alnylam-Advance-Zilebesiran-Into-Global-Phase-III-Cardiovascular-Outcomes-Trial-for-People-With-Uncontrolled-Hypertension/) - about 11,000 patients, 300 mg every six months vs placebo; KARDIA-3 missed its primary endpoint
- [EU CTIS: ZENITH trial record 2025-522960-34-00](https://ctis.eu/trial/2025-522960-34-00) - recruiting from Feb 2026 in 17 EU/EEA countries
- [Azizi et al., JAMA 2023: RADIANCE II ultrasound renal denervation](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2801849) - daytime ambulatory systolic pressure 6.3 mmHg lower than sham at 2 months
- [CI Today: CMS finalises national coverage for renal denervation (Oct 2025)](https://citoday.com/news/cms-finalizes-national-coverage-determination-for-renal-denervation-systems-1) - Symplicity Spyral and Paradise, both FDA-approved Nov 2023
- [Williams et al., Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol 2017: PASO outcomes after adrenalectomy (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28576687/) - 705 patients: 94% complete biochemical success, 37% complete clinical success, 47% partial
- [Endocrine Society guideline: Management of primary aldosteronism (2016, PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26934393/) - primary aldosteronism is common among people with hypertension (above 5%, possibly above 10%); surgery for unilateral disease, mineralocorticoid antagonists for bilateral

## Research log

- 2026-11-05: Baseline research for the first Cure Watch record (level, treatment, variants, pipeline, forecasts). Split into primary and secondary high blood pressure because secondary causes can sometimes be cured by surgery while primary cannot; resistant high blood pressure kept inside primary because its outlook is still lifelong control.

## Open questions

- The secondary variant's burden is rated heavy because its cure is surgery, but many people with secondary causes are treated with pills alone; moderate could also be argued.
- Estimates of how common secondary causes are range widely (about 5-20%) because screening is patchy; the guideline's 10% is used.
- Rich-country control rates depend on the target (under 140/90 vs under 130/80); CDC's 1 in 4 uses the stricter 2017 target.
