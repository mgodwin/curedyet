# Measles: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO fact sheet: Measles (15 Jul 2026)](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/measles) - about 95,000 deaths in 2024, mostly unvaccinated children under 5; no specific treatment; vitamin A two doses for children under 5; complications; nearly 59 million deaths averted 2000-2024
- [WHO/CDC global measles report coverage (Nov 2025)](https://epinews.emphnet.net/en/news/measles-deaths-down-88-since-2000-but-cases-surge) - about 11 million infections in 2024; deaths down 88% since 2000
- [WHO fact sheet: Immunization coverage](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/immunization-coverage) - 2025: 84% first measles dose, 77% second dose
- [CDC: Measles cases and outbreaks](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html) - 4,852 US cases and 43 new outbreaks in 2026 as of Oct 29; 2,289 cases in 2025
- [CDC: Measles clinical overview](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/hcp/clinical-overview/index.html) - no FDA-approved antiviral; vitamin A for severe cases; ribavirin unproven; 1 in 1,000 encephalitis; 1 to 3 in 1,000 children die; SSPE 7-10 years later; immune amnesia
- [CDC: MMR vaccine considerations](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/hcp/vaccine-considerations/index.html) - two doses about 97% effective, one dose about 93%; MMR within 72 hours or immunoglobulin within 6 days after exposure
- [CDC Pink Book: Measles chapter](https://www.cdc.gov/pinkbook/hcp/table-of-contents/chapter-13-measles.html) - vaccine licensed 1963 (Edmonston B, frequent fever and rash); further attenuated Edmonston-Enders strain 1968; MMR 1971; two-dose recommendation 1989; elimination declared 2000; immunity probably lifelong
- [CDC MMWR: Progress toward measles elimination, worldwide, 2000-2023](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/73/wr/mm7345a4.htm) - by end 2023, 82 (42%) countries verified as having eliminated measles; no WHO region had sustained elimination; no African Region country verified
- [PAHO: Update on the review of measles elimination status (2 Mar 2026)](https://www.paho.org/en/news/2-3-2026-update-review-measles-elimination-status) - Canada re-established endemic transmission and the Americas lost elimination status in Nov 2025; US and Mexico review moved to the Commission's November 2026 meeting
- [KFF: Measles elimination status, what it is and how the US could lose it](https://www.kff.org/other-health/measles-elimination-status-what-it-is-and-how-the-u-s-could-lose-it/) - 12 months of continuous transmission of one chain ends elimination
- [Local Government Association: UK loses measles elimination status (26 Jan 2026)](https://www.local.gov.uk/about/news/uk-loses-measles-elimination-status-lga-response) - WHO confirmed the UK lost its status
- [Global News: European countries lose measles status (Jan 2026)](https://globalnews.ca/news/11641263/european-countries-lose-measles-status) - Armenia, Austria, Azerbaijan, Spain, UK and Uzbekistan lost status
- [WHO/Europe and UNICEF: measles cases dropped in 2025 (11 Feb 2026)](https://www.who.int/europe/news/item/11-02-2026-measles-cases-dropped-in-europe-and-central-asia-in-2025-compared-to-the-previous-year--but-the-risk-of-outbreaks-remains---unicef-and-who) - 127,412 cases in 2024 and 33,998 in 2025; countries with endemic or re-established transmission rose to 19 from 12
- [Wittwer et al., Nature Communications 2021: polymerase inhibitor protects non-human primates from measles](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-25497-4) - ERDRP-0519 prevented disease in squirrel monkeys after exposure
- [Nature Communications 2024: GHP-88309 in ferrets](https://link.springer.com/10.1038/s41467-024-45418-5) - better oral efficacy than ERDRP-0519; wider protection window
- [Adigweme et al., Lancet 2024: measles-rubella microneedle patch in The Gambia](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)00532-4) - Phase 1/2; over 90% of infants protected against measles after one patch
- [Contagion Live: Measles update, November 6, 2026](https://www.contagionlive.com/view/measles-update-november-6-2026) - 5,182 US cases through Nov 5, 43 outbreaks
- [PAHO: Measles and rubella elimination regional monitoring and re-verification commission (14 Oct 2026)](https://www.paho.org/en/news/14-10-2026-measles-and-rubella-elimination-regional-monitoring-and-re-verification-commission) - meeting Nov 10-13, 2026

## Research log

- 2026-11-09: Updated the US count to 5,182 cases through Nov 5 (CDC via Contagion Live). PAHO's commission meets Nov 10-13 to classify elimination status; set next_milestone to that meeting. Forecasts unchanged.
- 2026-11-05: First full record. Rated the cure ladder on treatment (supportive care and vitamin A, no antiviral) and the prevention ladder on MMR. Chose prevention rung 3, not 4: only 82 of 194 countries (42%) were verified measles-free at the end of 2023, and since then Canada (Nov 2025) and six European countries including the UK and Spain (Jan 2026) lost status, the Americas lost regional status, and the US decision is due at PAHO's November 2026 meeting.

## Open questions

- PAHO's November 2026 Regional Verification Commission meeting will decide whether the US and Mexico have lost elimination status; update the prevention why and recent list after it.
- The cure ladder fits measles awkwardly: most patients recover without any drug, so level 4 is read here as an antiviral or antibody that clears infection and prevents complications for most patients. A reviewer may prefer a different reading.
- Confirm whether ERDRP-0519 or GHP-88309 has entered a Phase 1 trial; no registry entry was found.
