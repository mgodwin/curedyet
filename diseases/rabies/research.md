# Rabies: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO rabies fact sheet (Sept 2026)](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/rabies)
- [CDC: Rabies around the world](https://www.cdc.gov/rabies/location/world/)
- [Healio: CDC warns clinicians of surge in rabies activity (Sept 2026)](https://www.healio.com/news/infectious-disease/20260911/cdc-warns-clinicians-of-surge-in-rabies-activity)
- [A combination of two human monoclonal antibodies cures symptomatic rabies](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7645379)
- [HZAU: brain-penetrant antibody therapy for rabies (PNAS)](https://hzaml.hzau.edu.cn/info/1069/8043.htm)
- [Manifold: Cure for symptomatic rabies by 2040](https://manifold.markets/event/cure-for-symptomatic-rabies-by-2040)
- [Utah Department of Health: Rabies PEP fact sheet](https://epi.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/Rabies_PEP_fact_sheet.pdf) - PEP 100% effective if given properly and soon after exposure; schedule
- [WHO position on rabies immunization, 2018 updates](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6863036/) - Shortened PEP and PrEP schedules
- [Rabies PEP access, procurement and distribution in Asia and Africa, 2017-2018](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10351478/) - Vaccine free and consistently available in only 9 of 23 countries; RIG access gaps
- [CDC stacks: rabies vaccine review](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/41548) - HDCV licensed in the US on 9 June 1980
- [PAHO: rabies persists in only four countries of Latin America and the Caribbean (Sept 2018)](https://www.paho.org/en/news/21-9-2018-rabies-persists-only-four-countries-latin-america-and-caribbean) - 95% decline in dog and human rabies since 1983 via dog vaccination
