# Fatty liver disease (MASLD/MASH): research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Research log

- 2026-11-05: First full record. Split into three variants (simple fatty liver, MASH before cirrhosis, MASH cirrhosis) because outlook and treatment differ sharply: simple fat rarely harms the liver, MASH with F2-F3 scarring now has two approved drugs, and cirrhosis has none and carries real risk of liver failure and death. Overall level 2 for the typical patient, who has simple fatty liver or non-cirrhotic MASH and lives a near-normal life but needs ongoing weight control or drugs.

## Open questions

- Simple fatty liver is rated level 3 by analogy with early type 2 diabetes remission: weight loss clears the fat for a meaningful minority, but there is no good figure for how many keep it clear. Level 2 would also be defensible.
- MASH could arguably be level 3, since bariatric surgery or 10%+ weight loss resolves it for most who achieve them; rated 2 because few patients reach that and the drugs are ongoing.
- Exact ESSENCE resolution figures (62.9% vs 34.3% in NEJM) were checked only via rounded secondary reports (63% vs 34%).
- Efinopegdutide figures come from a news headline; the AASLD 2026 abstract was not read directly.
- No dated readout found for pegozafermin ENLIGHTEN-Fibrosis or survodutide LIVERAGE; check Roche and Boehringer updates.
- MAESTRO-NASH OUTCOMES timing (2027) is from February 2025 guidance and the trial is event-driven, so it may slip.
- The 38% global figure uses the older NAFLD definition; MASLD-defined estimates are similar but not identical.
