# Typhoid fever: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO fact sheet: Typhoid](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/typhoid) - cases, deaths, treatment, resistance
- [CDC Yellow Book: Typhoid and paratyphoid fever](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/travel-associated-infections-diseases/typhoid-and-paratyphoid-fever.html) - treatment and XDR strains
- [Gavi: typhoid conjugate vaccines are highly effective in African children](https://www.gavi.org/vaccineswork/now-we-know-typhoid-conjugate-vaccines-are-highly-effective-african-children) - 84% efficacy in Malawi trial
