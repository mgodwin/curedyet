# RSV (respiratory syncytial virus): research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [Li et al., Lancet 2022: Global burden of RSV in children under 5 (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35598608/) - 101,400 RSV-attributable deaths in children under 5 in 2019; over 97% in low- and middle-income countries
- [CDC: RSV clinical overview](https://www.cdc.gov/rsv/hcp/clinical-overview/index.html) - 2-3% of infants under 6 months hospitalised with RSV every year; most common cause of infant hospitalisation; vaccination for all adults 75+ and at-risk adults 50-74
- [CDC MMWR 2023: Use of RSV vaccines in older adults (ACIP)](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/72/wr/mm7229a4.htm) - 60,000-160,000 hospitalisations and 6,000-10,000 deaths a year in adults 65+; trial efficacy against RSV lower respiratory disease: Arexvy 82.6% season 1 and 74.5% over two seasons, Abrysvo 88.9% and 84.4%
- [CDC: RSV immunization guidance for infants and young children (Aug 2025)](https://www.cdc.gov/rsv/hcp/vaccine-clinical-guidance/infants-young-children.html) - nirsevimab 80.6% and clesrovimab 90.9% efficacy against RSV hospitalisation; infants under 8 months get an antibody unless protected by maternal vaccination
- [Lancet Child & Adolescent Health 2025 meta-analysis of nirsevimab real-world effectiveness (EurekAlert)](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1082345) - 27 studies, about 83% lower odds of RSV hospitalisation in the 2023-24 season
- [BioSpace: FDA approves Pfizer's maternal RSV vaccine (Aug 2023)](https://www.biospace.com/pfizer-wins-fda-approval-for-first-maternal-rsv-shot-to-protect-infants) - Abrysvo in pregnancy: 81.8% against severe RSV illness in the first 90 days, 69.4% at 180 days
- [CDC: RSV vaccine guidance for pregnant people](https://www.cdc.gov/rsv/hcp/vaccine-clinical-guidance/pregnant-people.html) - one dose of Abrysvo at 32-36 weeks, September through January in most of the US
- [WHO prequalifies first maternal RSV vaccine (Mar 2025)](https://www.who.int/news/item/19-03-2025-who-prequalifies-first-maternal-respiratory-syncytial-virus-vaccine) - prequalification opens Gavi and UNICEF purchasing; WHO recommends maternal vaccine from week 28 or nirsevimab at birth
- [Medical Letter: RSV vaccines for adults (2026)](https://secure.medicalletter.org/TML-article-1713d) - mResvia about 50% over 18 months, less durable than Arexvy or Abrysvo; Arexvy 67.7% over 23 months
- [Contagion Live: RSV vaccines show sustained effectiveness in older adults (Nov 2025)](https://www.contagionlive.com/view/rsv-vaccines-demonstrate-sustained-effectiveness-for-older-adults) - real-world IVY network study: 58% against hospitalisation over two seasons; 69% in the first season and 48% in the second
- [Pharmacy Times: FDA approves Arexvy for at-risk adults 18-49 (Mar 2026)](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/fda-approves-gsk-s-rsv-vaccine-for-adults-aged-18-49-at-increased-risk) - label expansion
- [Contagion Live: zelicapavir Phase 2b at IDWeek 2026 (Oct 2026)](https://www.contagionlive.com/view/novel-rsv-therapy-addressing-a-clinical-need) - missed primary endpoint; hospitalisation 1.7% vs 5.0%; no antiviral approved to treat RSV; RESOLVE Phase 2b/3 starting Q4 2026
- [Enanta: RSV pipeline](https://www.enanta.com/pipeline/respiratory-syncytial-virus/) - paediatric Phase 2b of zelicapavir with topline data expected 2027
- [ArkBio: ziresovir Phase 3 results published in NEJM](https://prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/arkbio-announces-publication-of-phase-3-clinical-trial-results-of-ziresovir-for-the-treatment-of-rsv-infection-in-the-new-england-journal-of-medicine-302259902.html) - AIRFLO trial in 311 hospitalised infants and young children in China
- [ArkBio: NMPA accepts ziresovir NDA with priority review (Dec 2022)](https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/12/09/2570754/0/en/Ark-Biopharmaceutical-Announces-NMPA-Acceptance-and-Priority-Review-of-New-Drug-Application-for-Ziresovir-for-Treatment-of-RSV-Infection.html) - filing status in China
- [Cochrane review: ribavirin for RSV in infants and young children](https://cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.CD000181.pub2/full) - trials too small to show reliable benefit; inhaled ribavirin licensed but rarely used
- [CIDRAP: paediatric RSV vaccine trials on hold (Dec 2024)](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/respiratory-syncytial-virus-rsv/pediatric-rsv-vaccine-trials-hold-fda-says) - severe RSV cases in infants given experimental mRNA vaccines paused infant vaccine trials

## Research log

## Open questions

- Level 1 is a judgement call: no antiviral is routinely used, so it could be argued as level 0, but supportive hospital care clearly eases severe illness.
- The prevention rung (partial) sits near the 85% line for infants: trial and real-world protection against hospitalisation is about 80-90%, but lower against milder illness, and older-adult protection wanes over two seasons. Rated for infants and older adults together.
- Ziresovir's NMPA approval status in China was not confirmed; it is listed as filed.
