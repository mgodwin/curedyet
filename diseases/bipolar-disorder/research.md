# Bipolar disorder: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO fact sheet: Bipolar disorder](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/bipolar-disorder) - 36 million people in 2023 (about 0.4%); die on average 13 years earlier; treatments (mood stabilisers, antipsychotics, CBT, interpersonal therapy, psychoeducation, family psychoeducation); low treatment coverage, especially in low- and middle-income countries; respect and shared decision-making
- [NIMH: Bipolar disorder statistics](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/statistics/bipolar-disorder) - US adults: 4.4% lifetime, 2.8% past year, 82.9% of past-year cases with serious impairment (NCS-R 2001-2003)
- [NIMH: Bipolar disorder health topic](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/bipolar-disorder) - shifts in mood and energy; usually requires lifelong treatment
- [NHS: Bipolar disorder treatment](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/conditions/bipolar-disorder/treatment/) - cannot be cured but can be managed; some medicines not safe in pregnancy; CBT
- [Perlis et al., Am J Psychiatry 2006: predictors of recurrence in STEP-BD](https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.ajp.163.2.217) - of 858 people who recovered, 48.5% had a recurrence within up to 2 years (34.7% depressive, 13.8% manic, hypomanic or mixed)
- [Geddes et al., Am J Psychiatry 2004: long-term lithium therapy meta-analysis](https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.ajp.161.2.217) - lithium reduces relapse versus placebo, clearest for mania
- [Cipriani et al., BMJ 2013: lithium in the prevention of suicide in mood disorders](https://read.qxmd.com/read/23814104/lithium-in-the-prevention-of-suicide-in-mood-disorders-updated-systematic-review-and-meta-analysis) - lithium effective in reducing suicide risk in mood disorders
- [Gitlin 2016: lithium side effects and toxicity (PubMed 27900734)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27900734/) - effects on kidneys, thyroid and parathyroid; periodic blood tests needed
- [J&J: Caplyta shows rapid improvement in bipolar mania in pivotal Phase 3 (21 Sep 2026)](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/caplyta-lumateperone-shows-significant-and-rapid-improvement-in-bipolar-mania-in-pivotal-phase-3-study) - Study 451: 4.8-point YMRS gain, 45.8% vs 20.9% response; Study 452 completed; approved for bipolar I and II depression
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07140913: adjunctive KarXT in bipolar I mania](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07140913) - Phase 3, recruiting, about 424 participants, estimated primary completion 28 June 2027
- [Psychiatric Times: positive Phase 2 results for RAP-219 in bipolar mania (20 Oct 2026)](https://www.psychiatrictimes.com/view/positive-topline-results-from-phase-2-trial-of-rap-219-for-the-treatment-of-bipolar-mania) - 253 patients, 3.1-point YMRS gain at week 3 (p=0.045); Phase 3 start H2 2027
- [HCPLive: FDA approves milsaperidone for acute bipolar I disorder and schizophrenia (Feb 2026)](https://www.hcplive.com/view/fda-approves-milsaperidone-acute-bipolar-i-disorder-schizophrenia) - Bysanti approval based on bioequivalence to iloperidone

## Research log

- 2026-11-05: First full record. Kept as a single record rather than splitting bipolar I and II: both are lifelong and recurrent, treated with the same families of medicines (mood stabilisers, some antipsychotics) plus therapy, and would sit at the same level with the same burden. The main differences (mania needing hospital care in bipolar I; depression dominating in bipolar II) change which drug is used for an episode, not the outlook for cure.

## Open questions

- No good long-term figure for the share of people who stay episode-free for many years on treatment; the level relies on STEP-BD recurrence and WHO's statements.
- Lithium's effect on suicide is supported by meta-analyses but rests on few events in trials; a 2022 meta-analysis found the trial data inconclusive.
- NIMH's 4.4% lifetime figure covers the whole bipolar spectrum, including milder subthreshold forms.
- Whether J&J will file lumateperone for mania, and when, is not public.
