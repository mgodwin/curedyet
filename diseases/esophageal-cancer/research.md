# Esophageal cancer: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [IARC GLOBOCAN 2024 fact sheet: Oesophagus](https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/globocan/factsheets/cancers/6-oesophagus-fact-sheet.pdf) - 494,320 new cases and 441,994 deaths in 2024; 13th for incidence, 7th for mortality
- [Morgan et al., Global landscape of esophageal squamous cell carcinoma and adenocarcinoma, Gastroenterology 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35671803/) - in 2020, 85% of cases were squamous and 14% adenocarcinoma; highest rates in Eastern Asia and Southern and Eastern Africa
- [NCI SEER Cancer Stat Facts: Esophageal cancer](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/esoph.html) - 22,530 US cases and 16,290 deaths est. 2026; lifetime risk 0.5% (2021-2023); 5-year relative survival 22.2% overall, 48.6% localized (19% of cases), 29.1% regional (32%), 5.3% distant (39%), 2016-2022
- [Survival | Oesophageal cancer | Cancer Research UK](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/oesophageal-cancer/survival) - net survival by stage, England, diagnosed 2016-2020 (NHS England): stage 1 almost 65%, stage 2 ~30%, stage 3 ~20% at 5 years; stage 4 5% at 4 years
- [NCI PDQ: Esophageal cancer treatment (health professional)](https://www.cancer.gov/types/esophageal/hp/esophageal-treatment-pdq) - adenocarcinoma at least 50% and squamous about 30% of US cases, squamous predominant worldwide; squamous linked to tobacco and alcohol, adenocarcinoma to reflux and Barrett esophagus; definitive chemoradiation for squamous; CROSS median OS squamous 81.6 vs 21.1 months, adenocarcinoma 43.2 vs 27.1
- [NCI PDQ: Esophageal cancer treatment (patient)](https://www.cancer.gov/types/esophageal/patient/esophageal-treatment-pdq) - stage definitions, endoscopic resection, esophagectomy, palliation of swallowing
- [ACS: Key statistics for esophageal cancer](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/esophagus-cancer/about/key-statistics.html) - much more common in Iran, northern China, India and southern Africa; US rates slowly falling
- [Eyck et al., CROSS 10-year outcome, J Clin Oncol 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33891478/) - preoperative chemoradiotherapy: 10-year OS 38% vs 25% (HR 0.70)
- [Hoeppner et al., ESOPEC, NEJM 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39842010/) - adenocarcinoma: 3-year OS 57.4% FLOT vs 50.7% CROSS (HR 0.70); grade 3+ adverse events 58% vs 50%
- [Kelly et al., CheckMate 577, NEJM 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33789008/) - adjuvant nivolumab for up to 1 year after CROSS and surgery with residual disease doubled median disease-free survival
- [Targeted Oncology: adjuvant nivolumab shows sustained benefit (Jul 2025)](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/adjuvant-nivolumab-shows-sustained-benefit-in-esophageal-gej-cancer) - CheckMate 577 final: 5-year OS 46% vs 41%, not statistically significant
- [van der Wilk et al., SANO trial, Lancet Oncol 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40112851/) - active surveillance after clinical complete response: 2-year OS 74% vs 71% with surgery, non-inferior
- [Kato et al., CheckMate 648 5-year follow-up, Ann Oncol 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42575473/) - advanced squamous: nivolumab plus chemotherapy or ipilimumab vs chemotherapy, OS HR 0.62 (PD-L1 1% or more)
- [Sun et al., KEYNOTE-590, Lancet 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34454674/) - pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy first-line for advanced esophageal cancer
- [Targeted Oncology: tislelizumab FDA approval in advanced esophageal cancer](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/tislelizumab-gains-fda-nod-in-advanced-esophageal-cancer) - first-line PD-L1-positive squamous cancer
- [CancerNetwork: FDA limits pembrolizumab and nivolumab in PD-L1-low disease (2025)](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/fda-limits-pembrolizumab-nivolumab-in-pd-l1-low-gastric-cancer-indications) - first-line labels limited to PD-L1 1 or more, including squamous esophageal cancer for nivolumab
- [ecancer: FDA approves zanidatamab and tislelizumab for HER2-positive gastric, GEJ or oesophageal adenocarcinoma (Aug 2026)](https://ecancer.org/en/news/28775-fda-approves-zanidatamab-hrii-and-tislelizumab-jsgr-for-her2-positive-gastric-gastro-oesophageal-junction-or-oesophageal-adenocarcinoma)
- [OncoDaily: Top gastroesophageal cancer updates from ESMO 2026](https://oncodaily.com/gastrointestinal-oncology/esmo-2026-gec) - KEYNOTE-975: pembrolizumab with definitive chemoradiation, EFS HR 0.97 and OS HR 1.00 (PD-L1 CPS 10 or more); study stopped
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04460352: NEEDS trial](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04460352) - definitive chemoradiation with salvage surgery as needed vs neoadjuvant chemoradiation and surgery, squamous; ~1,020 patients; primary completion est. Dec 31, 2026
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04550260: KUNLUN](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04550260) - durvalumab vs placebo with definitive chemoradiation, unresectable squamous; ~600 patients; primary completion est. June 2027
- [Effect of a computer-aided device for detecting gastric neoplasms, multicenter RCT (2026)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41801173/) - AI did not raise pathology-confirmed detection

## Open questions

- Not split by type: squamous and adenocarcinoma differ in cause, geography and how well they respond to chemoradiation, but registry stage survival is pooled and the outlook is similarly poor for both, so the differences are in the text and caveats. A split may be worth it if type-specific stage survival becomes available.
- Stage figures (England 2016-2020; SEER 2016-2022) predate FLOT replacing CROSS for many adenocarcinomas and adjuvant nivolumab's wide use.
- The 85% squamous share is for 2020 (Morgan et al.); the 2024 GLOBOCAN fact sheet does not split by type.
- Stage 0 (high-grade dysplasia in Barrett esophagus) is treated endoscopically but not split out, because registries rarely report survival for it.
- KUNLUN's results-reporting date on trial trackers (Nov 2026) is earlier than its listed primary completion (June 2027); the registry date is used.
