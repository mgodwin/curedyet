# Thalassemia: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [Modell & Darlison, Bull WHO 2008: global epidemiology of haemoglobin disorders](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2647473/) - Minimum estimates: 40,618 beta-thalassaemia births/yr, 25,511 transfusion-dependent; ~22,500 annual deaths because not transfused; 39% of known patients adequately chelated worldwide (19% South-East Asia, 27% Eastern Mediterranean)
- [Colah et al., Expert Rev Hematol 2010: global burden of beta-thalassemias and HbE disorders](https://informahealthcare.com/doi/full/10.1586/ehm.09.74) - About 1.5% of the world's population carries beta-thalassemia trait; birth estimates may be underestimates
- [MedlinePlus Genetics: beta thalassemia](https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/condition/beta-thalassemia/) - Most common in Mediterranean, North Africa, Middle East, India, Central and Southeast Asia
- [NHLBI: thalassemia treatment](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/thalassemia/treatment) - Transfusions every 3-4 weeks for beta-thalassemia major, occasional for HbH disease and intermedia; chelation drugs; few find a matched donor for transplant
- [Locatelli et al., NEJM 2024: exa-cel for transfusion-dependent beta-thalassemia (CLIMB THAL-111)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38657265/) - 32 of 35 evaluable (91%) transfusion independent for 12+ months; myeloablative busulfan conditioning; no deaths or cancers in interim analysis
- [Frangoul et al., NEJM 2026: exa-cel in children 5-11](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42274009/) - 8 of 8 TDT children followed 16+ months transfusion independent; all had grade 3-4 adverse events; 2 TDT children had severe busulfan-related veno-occlusive liver disease, 1 died
- [Pharmacy Times: FDA expands Casgevy to children 2 and older (Jul 2026)](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/fda-expands-casgevy-approval-to-children-as-young-as-2-years-with-sickle-cell-disease-or-transfusion-dependent--thalassemia) - Expanded US label for SCD and TDT, July 2026
- [BioSpace: FDA approves Casgevy for beta thalassemia (Jan 2024)](https://www.biospace.com/fda-approves-vertex-crispr-s-casgevy-to-treat-beta-thalassemia) - January 2024 TDT approval, ages 12+; widely reported $2.2M list price
- [BioPharma Dive: FDA approves Zynteglo (Aug 2022)](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/bluebird-bio-fda-approval-beti-cel-beta-thalassemia-zynteglo/629914/) - 89% transfusion independence in Phase 3; $2.8M US price; withdrawn from Europe in 2021 over pricing negotiations
- [Sickle Cell Anemia News: Casgevy approved in Bahrain (Dec 2023)](https://sicklecellanemianews.com/news/sickle-cell-gene-editing-therapy-approvaed-bahrain/) - First Middle East approval for SCD and TDT
- [Baronciani et al., Bone Marrow Transplant 2016: EBMT thalassemia transplant registry](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26752139/) - 1,493 patients 2000-2010; matched sibling OS 91%, thalassemia-free survival 83%; best results before age 14
- [Forni et al., Am J Hematol 2023: survival in beta-thalassemia major over 50 years](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36588408/) - 709 Italian patients born 1970-1997; 30-year survival 83.6% (oldest cohort) to 93.3% (youngest); heart disease main cause of death
- [Cappellini et al., NEJM 2020: BELIEVE luspatercept trial (Targeted Oncology summary)](https://targetedonc.com/view/transfusion-burden-reduction-seen-with-luspatercept-in-patients-with-transfusiondependent-betathalassemia) - 21.4% vs 4.5% reached at least 33% lower transfusion burden in weeks 13-24
- [Thalassaemia International Federation: EU approves luspatercept for NTDT beta-thalassaemia](https://thalassaemia.org.cy/news/top-story-eu-approves-luspatercept-for-anemia-in-patients-with-non-transfusion-dependent-%ce%b2-thalassaemia/) - EU authorization based on BEYOND (77% vs 0% hemoglobin response); CHMP opinion Jan 2023
- [Managed Healthcare Executive: FDA priority review of luspatercept in NTDT](https://www.managedhealthcareexecutive.com/view/fda-accepts-for-priority-review-supplemental-application-of-reblozyl) - US NTDT application (later withdrawn by BMS in 2022)
- [Agios via BioSpace: FDA approves Aqvesme (mitapivat) for thalassemia (Dec 2025)](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/u-s-fda-approves-agios-aqvesme-mitapivat-for-the-treatment-of-anemia-in-adults-with-alpha-or-beta-thalassemia) - First drug for anemia in both TDT and NTDT alpha- or beta-thalassemia; boxed warning for liver injury and REMS liver testing
- [HCPLive: FDA approves mitapivat for thalassemia](https://www.hcplive.com/view/fda-approves-mitapivat-aqvesme-for-non-transfusion-dependent-alpha--and-beta-thalassemia) - ENERGIZE: 55 of 130 vs 1 of 64 hemoglobin response; ENERGIZE-T: 30.4% vs 12.6% transfusion reduction response
- [Business Wire: positive CHMP opinion for Pyrukynd in thalassemia (Oct 2025)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251017844062/en/) - EU recommendation for adults with TDT and NTDT alpha- or beta-thalassemia; EC approval followed in 2026
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04987489: etavopivat in thalassemia or SCD (GLADIOLUS)](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04987489) - Phase 2, completed Sept 2025; EHA 2026 results reported by TIF (about 50% NTDT hemoglobin response at week 12)
- [ASH Annual Meeting 2026](https://www.hematology.org/meetings/annual-meeting) - December 12-15, 2026, New Orleans

## Research log

## Open questions

- Global birth and access figures are 2008 minimum estimates (Modell & Darlison); a newer GBD 2021 thalassemia estimate would be better.
- Casgevy's TDT list price was not stated in Vertex's own TDT announcement; $2.2M is the widely reported figure.
- Exact European Commission approval date for mitapivat in thalassemia was not confirmed from an EMA page (reported as spring 2026).
- Etavopivat GLADIOLUS figures come from secondary EHA 2026 summaries, not the primary presentation.
- No dated thalassemia regulatory decision was found for 2027; the next milestone is a conference, not a decision.
- Number of people treated with Casgevy for TDT worldwide was not found.
