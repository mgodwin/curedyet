## Research log

- 2026-11-15: Full review. Roche ended tominersen after GENERATION HD2 lowered huntingtin without functional benefit, a reminder that lowering the protein has not yet been shown to slow symptoms. AMT-130 needs an FDA advisory committee before the May 1, 2027 decision. Brezosiran heads to a pivotal trial in 2027. Forecast unchanged: tominersen was not in the pipeline list, and AMT-130 is the main near-term driver.
- 2026-11-03: Baseline research for the first Cure Watch snapshot (level, treatment, pipeline, forecasts).

## Open questions
