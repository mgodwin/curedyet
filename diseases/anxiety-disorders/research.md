# Anxiety disorders: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO fact sheet: Anxiety disorders](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/anxiety-disorders) - About 470 million people with anxiety disorders in 2023; about 1 in 4 people in need (27.6%) get any treatment; CBT including exposure is best supported; SSRIs help adults; benzodiazepines not generally recommended
- [NIMH: Any anxiety disorder](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/statistics/any-anxiety-disorder) - 19.1% of US adults past year, 31.1% lifetime (NCS-R, 2001-2003); the toll ratio
- [NIMH: Generalized anxiety disorder](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/statistics/generalized-anxiety-disorder) - 2.7% past year, 5.7% lifetime among US adults
- [NIMH: Panic disorder](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/statistics/panic-disorder) - 2.7% past year, 4.7% lifetime among US adults
- [NIMH: Social anxiety disorder](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/statistics/social-anxiety-disorder) - 7.1% past year, 12.1% lifetime among US adults
- [NIMH: Specific phobia](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/statistics/specific-phobia) - 9.1% past year, 12.5% lifetime among US adults
- [Springer et al., Clin Psychol Rev 2018: Remission in CBT for adult anxiety disorders](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29576326/) - Mean remission 51.0% across 100 studies; social anxiety and OCD lowest; depends on definition
- [Loerinc et al., Clin Psychol Rev 2015: Response rates for CBT for anxiety disorders](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26319194/) - Response 49.5% post-treatment, 53.6% at follow-up across 87 studies
- [van Dis et al., JAMA Psychiatry 2020: Long-term outcomes of CBT for anxiety-related disorders](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31758858/) - At 12+ months CBT benefit small to medium for GAD and social anxiety, not significant for panic; relapse 0-14% at 3-12 months in the few trials reporting it
- [Batelaan et al., BMJ 2017: Relapse after antidepressant discontinuation in anxiety disorders](https://www.psychotraumanet.org/sites/default/files/documents/Risk%20of%20relapse%20after%20antidepressant%20discontinuation%20in%20anxiety%20disorders.pdf) - 28 studies, 5,233 people: relapse 36.4% after stopping vs 16.4% continuing (OR 3.11), up to 1 year
- [Odgers et al., Behav Res Ther 2022: Single- vs multi-session exposure for specific phobia](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36323055/) - 85 studies, 1,758 people; large effects at post-treatment and follow-up; single session as effective and more efficient
- [Zlomke and Davis, Behav Ther 2008: One-session treatment of specific phobias](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18721635/) - OST is massed exposure in one session of up to three hours; research generally supports efficacy
- [Ollendick et al., Behav Res Ther 2010 (PMC): Comorbidity in youth with specific phobias](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2914122/) - In the 2009 OST trial, clinician severity fell from about 6 to about 3.5 after treatment and about 3 at 6 months
- [Ollendick et al., J Consult Clin Psychol 2009: One-session treatment of specific phobias in youth](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19485591/) - RCT of 196 youths; OST beat education support on severity and share diagnosis-free; effects maintained
- [NICE CG113: Generalised anxiety disorder and panic disorder in adults](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg113) - Stepped care; high-intensity CBT usually 12-15 weekly sessions for GAD, 7-14 hours for panic; SSRI (sertraline first) then another SSRI or SNRI; benzodiazepines only short-term in crises for GAD and not for panic
- [Definium: Positive topline results from Phase 3 Voyage study of DT120 in GAD (Aug 12, 2026)](https://ir.definiumtx.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/243/definium-therapeutics-announces-positive-topline-results-from-phase-3-voyage-study-of-dt120-odt-in-generalized-anxiety-disorder) - 214 patients; single dose; HAM-A -11.6 vs -6.2 at week 12 (difference 5.4, d=0.81); generally well tolerated
- [Pharmaceutical Technology: Definium tees up regulatory submission after Panorama (Sep 15, 2026)](https://www.pharmaceutical-technology.com/news/definium-dt120-psychedelic-gad-anxiety-phase-iii/) - Panorama 100 µg 5.1-point HAM-A benefit at week 12; pre-NDA meeting Q4 2026; NDA planned 1H 2027; 40-week extension results not yet reported
- [Definium Q1 2026 results (SEC exhibit, May 7, 2026)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001813814/000119312526211929/dftx-ex99_1.htm) - DT120 trial timelines (Voyage, Panorama, Emerge, Ascend, Haven); MindMed renamed Definium, MM120 renamed DT120
- [Psychiatric Times: Fasedienol misses in PALISADE-3 (Dec 2025)](https://www.psychiatrictimes.com/view/fasedienol-for-social-anxiety-disorder-does-not-demonstrate-significant-improvement-in-phase-3-study) - No significant benefit over placebo in a public-speaking challenge
- [Vistagen: PALISADE-4 topline (Jun 30, 2026)](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/vistagen-announces-topline-post-hoc-120000253.html) - Missed primary endpoint; plans FDA discussion of a multi-dose Phase 3 with LSAS endpoint
- [Big Health: FDA grants clearance for DaylightRx (Sep 2024)](https://www.bighealth.com/news/us-fda-grants-clearance-for-daylightrx) - First FDA-cleared digital treatment for GAD; prescription CBT program, adjunct to usual care
- [Dartmouth: First therapy chatbot trial yields mental health benefits (Mar 2025)](https://tto.dartmouth.edu/news/2025/03/first-therapy-chatbot-trial-yields-mental-health-benefits) - Therabot RCT (NEJM AI) in 210 adults; greater symptom drops for depression, anxiety and eating concerns vs waitlist at 4 weeks

## Open questions

- Level 2 vs 3 for generalized, panic and social anxiety: CBT is time-limited and about half reach remission, which is close to "durable remission for a meaningful subset", but long-term (12+ month) effects are small and natural-course studies show frequent recurrence. Rated 2 for consistency with depression; revisit if long-term remission data improve.
- Specific phobia: no good pooled estimate of the share diagnosis-free long term after exposure; rated 3, not 4, on that uncertainty.
- PTSD and OCD are no longer classed as anxiety disorders in DSM-5 and are left out here.
- DT120: durability past 12 weeks (Panorama extension, Voyage Part B) and FDA handling of functional unblinding are unknown; the FDA decision date in `expected` is an estimate from the planned 2027 filing.
- NIMH prevalence data are from a 2001-2003 survey; newer US diagnostic-interview data would be better.
