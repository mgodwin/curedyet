# Prion disease (CJD): research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

- [Brain-wide silencing of prion protein by AAV-delivered epigenetic editor](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11875203/)
- [BioXconomy: Oligo-based therapies lead charge against prion disease](https://www.bioxconomy.com/modalities/oligo-based-therapies-lead-charge-against-prion-disease)
- [ClinicalTrials.gov: PRiSM PrP-targeting siRNA (NCT07444580)](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07444580): Broad Institute, Phase 1 single ascending dose, symptomatic adults and presymptomatic carriers of highly penetrant PRNP variants, ~45 participants, start 2026-05-15, primary completion est. 2029-08-14.
- [Ionis: letter to the prion disease community, 25 Aug 2026](https://cjdsupport.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Ionis-prion-community-update-August-2026-vF.pdf): PrProfile no longer recruiting; safety and efficacy not established. Registry (NCT06153966): active, not recruiting, 85 enrolled, primary completion est. 2027-04.
- [CDC: About variant CJD](https://www.cdc.gov/variant-creutzfeldt-jakob/about/index.html): 233 vCJD illnesses worldwide since 1996, 4 in the US; linked to beef from BSE ("mad cow disease") cattle; most cases in the UK in the late 1990s and early 2000s; average age 28 vs 68; average illness 13-14 months; feed bans and removing brain and other high-risk tissue from food made BSE rare; no specific vCJD precautions needed now.
- [UK National CJD Research & Surveillance Unit: Variant CJD cases worldwide (data at 5 Jul 2022)](https://cjd.ed.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2025-01/Current%20data%20on%20variant%20CJD%20cases%20worldwide.pdf): UK 175 primary + 3 transfusion-linked = 178; worldwide 230 primary + 3 secondary; France 29, Spain 5, Ireland 4, USA 4, others 1-3.
- [Brown et al., Iatrogenic CJD, final assessment (Emerging Infectious Diseases, 2012)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3358170/): ~470 iatrogenic cases, mainly cadaver-derived growth hormone (226) and dura mater grafts (228); era nearly closed; recommends continued instrument and product decontamination.
- [Appleby et al., Genetic aspects of human prion diseases (Front Neurol, 2022)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9579322/): genetic prion disease is 10-15% of cases; gCJD usually <1 year, FFI usually >1 year, GSS ~5 years mean; predictive testing on the Huntington disease protocol; IVF with preimplantation genetic diagnosis lets carriers have children without the variant.
- [Cohort study of biomarkers in presymptomatic PRNP carriers (PMC7302371)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7302371/): seeding and neuronal damage markers do not separate presymptomatic carriers from controls, favouring primary prevention trials using PrP lowering as the measure.
- [Collinge et al., Kuru in the 21st century (Lancet, 2006)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(06)68879-0): kuru spread through mortuary feasts in Papua New Guinea that ended by about 1960; late cases had incubation periods that may exceed 50 years.

## Open questions

- The ION717 milestone comes from the researchers' blog (cureffi.org), not from Ionis; the registry gives primary completion April 2027.
- Rating acquired prevention as level 4 stretches a ladder built for vaccines and drugs: the protection is population-wide food and medical safeguards, not something a person takes. The reached year (2005) is approximate.
- Some acquired infections may still be silent given decades-long incubation; a few recent vCJD cases were in lab workers (CDC).
