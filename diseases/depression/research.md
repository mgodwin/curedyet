## Research log

- 2026-11-15: Full review. Compass completed the COMP360 NDA on Nov 9 (rolling review, priority voucher, no published decision date); Kalshi prices FDA approval at about 81% before July 2027. Xenon moved X-NOVA2 topline to Dec 2026 and paused new enrollment after a safety review. A Lancet Psychiatry network analysis (Nov 2026) ranked escitalopram, agomelatine and duloxetine best for lasting recovery. Psilocybin would add an option for treatment-resistant depression, not a cure for most, so the forecast is unchanged.
- 2026-11-03: Baseline research for the first Cure Watch snapshot (level, treatment, pipeline, forecasts).

## Open questions

- The FDA has not published a COMP360 decision date; DEA rescheduling timing is also unknown.
- Xenon did not state publicly why it paused depression enrollment beyond a safety board review of neuropsychiatric events.
