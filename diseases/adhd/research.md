# ADHD: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [CDC: ADHD data at a glance](https://www.cdc.gov/adhd/data/index.html) - 7 million US children ever diagnosed, 2022
- [CHADD: general prevalence of ADHD](https://www.chadd.org/understanding-adhd/about-adhd/data-and-statistics/general-prevalence/) - 7.2% of children, about 2.6% of adults worldwide
- [Psychiatric Times: FDA approves centanafadine (2026)](https://www.psychiatrictimes.com/view/fda-approves-centanafadine-for-adhd-in-children-adolescents-and-adults) - approval and trial basis
