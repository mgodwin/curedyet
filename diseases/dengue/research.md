# Dengue: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO fact sheet: Dengue and severe dengue (21 Aug 2025)](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/dengue-and-severe-dengue) - 14.6M cases and 12,000+ deaths in 2024; 100-400M infections a year; about half the world at risk; endemic in 100+ countries; no specific treatment; Qdenga recommended for ages 6-16 in high-transmission settings
- [CDC: Clinical features of dengue](https://www.cdc.gov/dengue/hcp/clinical-signs/index.html) - about 1 in 4 infections symptomatic; about 1 in 20 patients progress to severe dengue; prompt IV fluids cut severe dengue deaths to under 0.5%
- [CDC-authored study: Dengue deaths in Puerto Rico, 2007 epidemic (PLoS NTD)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3328431/) - better case management cut severe dengue mortality from 10-20% to under 1% in some countries
- [J&J: discontinuing Phase 2 field study of mosnodenvir (Oct 2024)](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/johnson-johnson-to-discontinue-phase-2-field-study-evaluating-investigational-antiviral-for-the-prevention-of-dengue) - study of the antiviral ended for portfolio reasons, not safety
- [Novartis clinical trial NCT06006559: EYU688 in dengue fever](https://www.novartis.com/clinicaltrials/study/nct06006559) - Phase 2, about 108 adults, recruiting, completion estimated July 2027
- [Takeda: TAK-003 protection through 4.5 years (Jun 2022)](https://www.takeda.com/newsroom/newsreleases/2022/takedas-dengue-vaccine-candidate-provides-continued-protection-against-dengue-fever-through-45-years-in-pivotal-clinical-trial/) - 61% against symptomatic dengue and 84% against hospitalisation over 4.5 years
- [Fierce Pharma: Takeda withdraws US filing for dengue vaccine (Jul 2023)](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/Takeda-yanks-fda-application-dengue-vaccine-citing-data-collection-disagreement) - Qdenga not licensed in the US
- [WHO position paper on dengue vaccines (May 2024)](https://www.who.int/westernpacific/publications/i/item/who-wer-9918-203-224) - TAK-003 for ages 6-16 in high-burden, high-transmission settings, two doses three months apart
- [The Week: India approves Qdenga for ages 4-60 (Jul 2026)](https://www.theweek.in/news/health/2026/07/20/how-effective-is-qdenga-india-approves-first-dengue-vaccine-by-japanese-drugmaker-for-ages-4-60.html) - first dengue vaccine in India
- [Center for Immunization Research: Butantan-DV approved by ANVISA](https://centerforimmunizationresearch.org/butantan-institutes-dengue-vaccine-the-worlds-first-single-dose-vaccine-has-been-approved-by-anvisa) - Brazil approval Nov 2025, single dose, ages 12-59
- [MercoPress: Brazil approves locally produced dengue vaccine (Nov 2025)](https://en.mercopress.com/2025/11/27/brazil-approves-locally-produced-dengue-vaccine) - approval and planned 30 million doses with WuXi Biologics in late 2026
- [Agência FAPESP: Butantan-DV Phase 3 results in NEJM (Feb 2024)](https://agencia.fapesp.br/50764) - Kallás et al.: 79.6% efficacy over two years; 90% in ages 18-59; DENV-3 and -4 not assessed
- [Kallás et al., Nature Medicine 2026: Long-term efficacy of single-dose Butantan dengue vaccine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04255-3) - five-year follow-up
- [Agência FAPESP: Butantan-DV 80.5% effective against severe cases after five years (Mar 2026)](https://agencia.fapesp.br/57407) - 65% against symptomatic dengue over five years; no vaccinated hospitalisations vs 8 on placebo
- [Diário de Pernambuco: Butantan brings forward delivery of dengue vaccines to SUS (Feb 2026)](https://www.diariodepernambuco.com.br/dp-/dp-saude/2026/02/11708416-butantan-antecipa-entrega-de-13-mi-de-vacinas-contra-dengue-ao-sus.html) - rollout to health workers from Feb 2026; up to 100 million doses by 2027
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07013487: V181 MOBILIZE-1](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07013487) - Merck single-dose vaccine, Phase 3 in about 12,000 children aged 2-17, primary completion Oct 2031
- [Immunize.org: dengue vaccine products](https://www.immunize.org/ask-experts/topic/dengue/vaccine-products-dengue/) - Dengvaxia restricted to ages 9-16 with confirmed past infection because it raises severe-dengue risk in people never infected
- [EMA: Dengvaxia EPAR](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/dengvaxia) - EU marketing authorisation withdrawn Oct 2025 at Sanofi's request
- [Utarini et al., NEJM 2021: Wolbachia-infected mosquito deployments for dengue control](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2030243) - releases cut symptomatic dengue by 77% and hospitalisation by 86% in Yogyakarta
- [WHO Weekly Epidemiological Record: global dengue update (2026)](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/who-wer10140-285-289) - 2026 case counts so far below 2025
- [Serum Institute of India: global trial of dengue antibody treatment (8 Jul 2026)](https://seruminstitute.com/brazil-malaysia-and-thailand-set-to-start-global-clinical-trial-to-test-promising-dengue-treatment-developed-by-serum-institute-of-india.php) - Phase 3, about 1,000 patients, start Q1 2027

## Research log

- 2026-11-09: Added the Serum Institute of India dengue antibody (formerly VIS513), in a pivotal Phase 3 in India, with a DNDi-led Phase 3 in Brazil, Malaysia and Thailand due to start in early 2027. No approvals or readouts this month; forecasts unchanged.
- 2026-11-05: Baseline research for the first Cure Watch record (level, treatment, prevention, pipeline, forecasts).

## Open questions

- Level 1 is a judgement call: no drug acts on the virus, so it could be argued as level 0, but good supportive care changes outcomes for severe dengue enough to count as easing it.
- Butantan-DV efficacy figures differ by follow-up: 79.6% at two years (NEJM 2024) vs 65% over five years (Nature Medicine 2026). The five-year figure is used for the rung.
- Wolbachia mosquito releases are vector control rather than a vaccine or drug, so they are listed but not used to set the rung.
- Mosnodenvir field-study results have not been published, as far as this review found.
- No efficacy figures have been published for the Serum Institute antibody; look for the India Phase 3 results.
