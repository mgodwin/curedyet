# Uterine (endometrial) cancer: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [NCI SEER Cancer Stat Facts: Uterine cancer](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/corp.html) - 68,270 US cases and 14,450 deaths est. 2026; 3.1% lifetime risk (2021-2023); stage at diagnosis 67% localized, 18% regional, 11% distant; 5-year relative survival 80.9% overall, 94.9% localized, 70.1% regional, 19.9% distant (2016-2022); incidence rising 0.7%/yr (2014-2023) and deaths 1.3%/yr (2015-2024); death rate 9.9 per 100,000 in Black vs 4.9 in White women with case rates 31.1 vs 27.7
- [GLOBOCAN 2024 corpus uteri fact sheet (IARC)](https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/globocan/factsheets/cancers/24-corpus-uteri-fact-sheet.pdf) - 434,620 new cases and 100,680 deaths worldwide in 2024
- [ACS: Key statistics for endometrial cancer](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/endometrial-cancer/about/key-statistics.html) - US 2026 estimates include uterine sarcomas; fourth most common cancer in US women; average age 60; incidence rising in non-White groups; death rate rising about 1.6%/yr; Black women's death rate nearly double White women's
- [ACS: Treatment of endometrial cancer by stage](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/endometrial-cancer/treating/by-stage.html) - hysterectomy with removal of tubes and ovaries; radiation and chemotherapy by stage and type; progestin fertility-sparing option for stage IA grade 1
- [Cancer Research UK: Womb cancer survival](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/womb-cancer/survival) - 5-year net survival by stage, England 2013-2017: stage 1 more than 90%, stage 2 around 75%, stage 3 almost 50%, stage 4 15%
- [Leon-Castillo et al., J Clin Oncol 2020: Molecular classification of the PORTEC-3 trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7527156) - high-risk tumors: p53abn 23%, POLEmut 12%, MMRd 33%, NSMP 32%; 5-year relapse-free survival 48%, 98%, 72% and 74%; chemotherapy benefit mainly in p53abn
- [Fremond et al., Lancet Digit Health 2023: im4MEC (Maastricht University record)](https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/en/publications/interpretable-deep-learning-model-to-predict-the-molecular-classi) - deep learning predicts molecular class from H&E slides (research use)
- [ASCO Post: FDA expands dostarlimab indication (Aug 2024)](https://ascopost.com/news/august-2024/fda-expands-endometrial-cancer-indication-for-dostarlimab-gxly-with-chemotherapy/) - RUBY OS HR 0.69; dMMR PFS HR 0.28; earlier July 2023 approval limited to dMMR/MSI-H
- [FDA approves pembrolizumab with chemotherapy for primary advanced or recurrent endometrial carcinoma (Jun 2024)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-pembrolizumab-chemotherapy-primary-advanced-or-recurrent-endometrial-carcinoma) - NRG-GY018 approval
- [FDA approves durvalumab with chemotherapy for dMMR primary advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer (Jun 2024)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-durvalumab-chemotherapy-mismatch-repair-deficient-primary-advanced-or-recurrent) - DUO-E, dMMR PFS HR 0.42
- [Merck: FDA approves pembrolizumab plus lenvatinib for certain advanced endometrial carcinoma (Jul 2021)](https://www.merck.com/news/fda-approves-keytruda-pembrolizumab-plus-lenvima-lenvatinib-combination-for-patients-with-certain-types-of-advanced-endometrial-carcinoma/) - KEYNOTE-775, after prior systemic therapy, not dMMR
- [OncoDaily: NRG-GY018 at ASCO 2026](https://oncodaily.com/trial-updates/nrg-gy018-trial-asco-2026) - dMMR 48-month OS 78.6% vs 60.4% (HR 0.56); pMMR median OS 46.9 vs 35.1 months (HR 0.86, not significant)
- [OncLive: Sac-TMT improves PFS and OS, under FDA priority review (Oct 2026)](https://www.onclive.com/view/sac-tmt-improves-pfs-and-os-is-under-fda-priority-review-in-previously-treated-endometrial-cancer) - TroFuse-005, 776 patients; median OS 19.0 vs 12.1 months (HR 0.68); PDUFA Jun 15, 2027
- [CURE: ESMO 2026 endometrial results](https://www.curetoday.com/view/3-esmo-2026-results-in-endometrial-and-breast-cancer-to-know) - KEYNOTE-C93: pembrolizumab alone vs chemotherapy in first-line dMMR, median PFS 38.2 vs 8.4 months, 3-year OS 70.7% vs 62.0% (not significant), serious treatment-related events 18.1% vs 50.7%
- [Kucera et al., JAMA Netw Open 2023: survival disparities between Black and White patients with uterine cancer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10111180/) - NCDB, 274,838 patients; 5-year survival 58.6% vs 78.5%; histologic subtype linked to about 54% of the gap; insurance about 11.5% under age 65
- [Eakin et al., Curr Opin Obstet Gynecol 2023: trends and disparities in high-risk endometrial cancer](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9812879) - 5-year relative survival 63% Black vs 84% White women, the largest Black-White gap of any cancer

## Research log

- 2026-11-05: First full record. Split into FIGO stages I-IV with England stage survival from Cancer Research UK (2013-2017) and US stage shares from SEER. Covered molecular classification, 2023-24 immunotherapy approvals, rising incidence and the US racial survival gap. Uterine sarcoma excluded.

## Open questions

- Stage survival data (England 2013-2017; SEER 2016-2022) predate immunotherapy with first-line chemotherapy (US approvals 2023-24) and the 2023 FIGO staging update that folds molecular class into stage; newer data could move stage III and the dMMR part of stage IV.
- Stage IV is rated 1 although mismatch repair-deficient tumors (roughly a quarter to a third) can be controlled for years on immunotherapy; if long-term data show many such women are cured, stage IV could arguably be rated 3, as melanoma stage IV is.
- SEER and ACS totals include uterine sarcomas (up to about 10% of uterine cancers by ACS); stage figures are not split by histology.
- ACS reports incidence leveling off overall and among White women, while SEER reports a 0.7%/yr rise over 2014-2023; the record uses SEER.
- Stage I reached year (1950) is approximate; no single source dates it.
