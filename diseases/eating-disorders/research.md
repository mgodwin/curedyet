# Eating disorders: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO fact sheet: Mental disorders](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/mental-disorders) - 16 million with eating disorders in 2021
- [Recovery, relapse and mortality in eating disorders: systematic review (2023)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10228434) - 46% recovery overall, 67% at 10+ years
