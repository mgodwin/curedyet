# Stomach cancer: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [IARC GLOBOCAN 2024 fact sheet: Stomach](https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/globocan/factsheets/cancers/7-stomach-fact-sheet.pdf) - 980,286 new cases and 641,554 deaths in 2024; 5th for incidence and mortality
- [ACS global cancer statistics 2026](https://pressroom.cancer.org/global-cancer-statistics-2026) - about 980,000 cases and 642,000 deaths (2024)
- [NCI SEER Cancer Stat Facts: Stomach cancer](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/stomach.html) - 31,510 US cases and 10,740 deaths est. 2026; lifetime risk 0.8% (2021-2023); 5-year relative survival 39.8% overall, 78.1% localized (32% of cases), 39.0% regional (23%), 8.1% distant (35%), 2016-2022
- [Survival | Stomach cancer | Cancer Research UK](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/stomach-cancer/survival) - 5-year net survival by stage, England, diagnosed 2013-2017 (ONS): stage 1 65%, stage 2 ~35%, stage 3 ~25%; stage 4 ~20% at 1 year
- [NCI PDQ: Stomach (gastric) cancer treatment](https://www.cancer.gov/types/stomach/patient/stomach-treatment-pdq) - stage definitions, endoscopic resection and gastrectomy
- [Park et al., Cancer statistics in Korea 2022, Cancer Res Treat 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12016816/) - stomach cancer screening rate 77.5% (2023); localized share 51.7% (2005) to 69.8% (2022); 5-year relative survival 78.4% (2018-2022); localized stomach cancer 5-year survival 98.1% (men) and 96.1% (women)
- [Hamashima, Japanese guidelines for gastric cancer screening, Jpn J Clin Oncol 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29889263/) - Japan's national endoscopic and radiographic screening
- [Park et al., Global lifetime estimates of preventable gastric cancers, Nat Med 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40624406/) - IARC: 15.6M lifetime gastric cancers expected among people born 2008-2017, 76% attributable to H. pylori; screen-and-treat could prevent up to 75%
- [Ford et al., H. pylori eradication to prevent gastric cancer, Gastroenterology 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39824392/) - meta-analysis: RR of gastric cancer 0.64 in healthy infected people (8 RCTs), 0.52 after endoscopic resection; RR of death 0.78
- [Choi et al., Family history of gastric cancer and H. pylori treatment, NEJM 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31995688/) - eradication in first-degree relatives lowered gastric cancer risk
- [Al-Batran et al., FLOT4, Lancet 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30982686/) - perioperative FLOT vs ECF/ECX: median OS 50 vs 35 months, HR 0.77
- [Janjigian et al., MATTERHORN, NEJM 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40454643/) - durvalumab plus FLOT: 2-year EFS 67.4% vs 58.5% (HR 0.71); pCR 19.2% vs 7.2%; grade 3-4 adverse events 72% vs 71%
- [Janjigian et al., MATTERHORN final OS, Lancet 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42716074/) - overall survival HR 0.78 (95% CI 0.63-0.96)
- [CancerNetwork: FDA approves durvalumab plus FLOT (Nov 2025)](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/fda-approves-durvalumab-plus-flot-in-resectable-gastric-gej-cancers) - approval Nov 25, 2025, not restricted by PD-L1
- [FDA: zolbetuximab-clzb approval (Oct 2024)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-zolbetuximab-clzb-chemotherapy-gastric-or-gastroesophageal-junction-adenocarcinoma) - first-line CLDN18.2-positive, HER2-negative; SPOTLIGHT median OS 18.2 vs 15.5 months, GLOW 14.4 vs 12.2
- [Shitara et al., SPOTLIGHT, Lancet 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37068504/) - zolbetuximab plus mFOLFOX6; grade 3+ nausea and vomiting most common
- [Janjigian et al., KEYNOTE-811, Lancet 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37871604/) - pembrolizumab plus trastuzumab and chemotherapy for HER2-positive disease
- [Janjigian et al., CheckMate 649 5-year follow-up, Ann Oncol 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41687718/) - nivolumab plus chemotherapy, PD-L1 CPS 5 or more: 5-year OS 16% vs 6% (HR 0.71)
- [CancerNetwork: FDA limits pembrolizumab and nivolumab in PD-L1-low gastric cancer (2025)](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/fda-limits-pembrolizumab-nivolumab-in-pd-l1-low-gastric-cancer-indications) - labels restricted to PD-L1 CPS 1 or more after May 2025 FDA letters
- [FDA: zanidatamab and tislelizumab approval for HER2-positive gastric/GEJ cancer (Aug 2026)](https://fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-zanidatamab-hrii-and-tislelizumab-jsgr-her2-positive-gastric-gastroesophageal-junction) - first-line HER2-positive, with or without tislelizumab
- [OncoDaily: Top gastroesophageal cancer updates from ESMO 2026](https://oncodaily.com/gastrointestinal-oncology/esmo-2026-gec) - HERIZON-GEA-01 OS 27.0 (triplet) vs 19.6 months; G-HOPE-001 arcotatug tavatecan OS 10.2 vs 6.9 months (HR 0.57); CLARITY-Gastric 01 sonesitatug vedotin OS 10.0 vs 5.0 months in third line; LOMAC 4 vs 6 months adjuvant XELOX non-inferior; DEWI dostarlimab 62.5% clinical complete response in dMMR disease
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06532006: HLX22 Phase 3](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06532006) - first-line HER2-positive, ~550 patients, primary completion est. June 2027
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07103668: CLDN18.2 CAR-T Phase 3](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07103668) - unresectable locally advanced disease, 150 patients, primary endpoint PFS, primary completion est. May 2027
- [Effect of a computer-aided device for detecting gastric neoplasms, multicenter RCT (2026)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41801173/) - 29,514 patients; AI did not raise pathology-confirmed detection (RR 1.13, P = .25) but cut blind spots

## Open questions

- Stage I is rated 3 on English (65%) and US SEER localized (78%) data, but Korea reports 96-98% for localized disease and Japan and Korea together diagnose a large share of rich-country cases. A pooled rich-country figure could put stage I at 4.
- Stage-specific English figures (2013-2017) predate perioperative FLOT and durvalumab; no registry yet reports AJCC stage survival for the current regimen.
- Stage 0 (carcinoma in situ) was not split out: it is rarely reported separately in Western registries.
