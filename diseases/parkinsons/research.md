## Research log

- 2026-11-15: Full review. At MDS 2026 (Oct) the GCase activator BIA 28-6156 failed phase 2b ACTIVATE in GBA-linked Parkinson's, the second disease-modifying failure this year after BIIB122 (LUMA). Solengepras met its phase 3 goal for off time, a symptom gain. Trimmed 2045 odds slightly (normal 10 to 9, faster 30 to 28), within the 2-point cap, because two of the main genetic-target bets failed; 2035 and years unchanged.
- 2026-11-03: Baseline research for the first Cure Watch snapshot (level, treatment, pipeline, forecasts).
