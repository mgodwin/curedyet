# Lyme disease: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [CDC: Lyme disease facts and stats](https://www.cdc.gov/lyme/data-research/facts-stats/index.html) - more than 80,000 cases reported a year; claims-based estimate of 476,000 treated a year, possibly an overestimate
- [US Census Bureau: Vintage 2024 population estimates (Dec 2024)](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2024/population-estimates-international-migration.html) - US population surpasses 340 million; denominator for the 1-in-700 ratio
- [Burn et al., Vector-Borne and Zoonotic Diseases 2023: Incidence of Lyme borreliosis in Europe from national surveillance systems (2005-2020)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10122224/) - on average 128,888 cases reported a year across 25 countries; underascertainment acknowledged (Pfizer-funded)
- [CDC: Treatment and intervention for Lyme disease](https://www.cdc.gov/lyme/treatment/index.html) - 10 days to 4 weeks of doxycycline, amoxicillin or cefuroxime; early treatment usually brings rapid and complete recovery
- [CDC clinical care: Erythema migrans rash](https://www.cdc.gov/lyme/hcp/clinical-care/erythema-migrans-rash.html) - doxycycline 10-14 days, amoxicillin or cefuroxime 14 days
- [CDC clinical care: Neurologic Lyme disease](https://www.cdc.gov/lyme/hcp/clinical-care/neurologic-lyme-disease.html) - 14-21 days oral doxycycline or IV ceftriaxone; untreated late disease can leave nerve damage
- [CDC clinical care: Lyme arthritis](https://www.cdc.gov/lyme/hcp/clinical-care/lyme-arthritis.html) - 28 days oral antibiotics; second oral course or IV ceftriaxone; persistent arthritis thought immunologic; about 1 in 4 reported cases
- [Tory, Zurakowski and Sundel, J Rheumatol 2010: Outcomes of children treated for Lyme arthritis](https://www.jrheum.org/content/37/5/1049) - in adults, arthritis persists despite antibiotics in about 10% of cases
- [Lochhead et al., Infect Dis Clin North Am 2022: Lyme arthritis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9533683/) - responses to antibiotics generally excellent; small share with postinfectious synovitis that responds to anti-inflammatory therapy
- [Steere, J Infect Dis 2024: Lyme arthritis, a 50-year journey](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11322885/) - recognised 1975, agent cultured 1982, arthritis usually treated successfully with oral antibiotics
- [Steere et al., Ann Intern Med 1983: Treatment of the early manifestations of Lyme disease](https://doi.org/10.7326/0003-4819-99-1-22) - 1980-82 trial; tetracycline-treated patients developed no major late complications
- [German S3 guideline: Lyme neuroborreliosis (GMS 2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12584707/) - 14 days of antibiotics sufficient in early neuroborreliosis; neurological manifestations in 3-15% of infections
- [CDC: Chronic symptoms and Lyme disease](https://www.cdc.gov/lyme/signs-symptoms/chronic-symptoms-and-lyme-disease.html) - PTLDS term; symptoms 5-10% more common at 6 months than in people never infected; cause unknown; extended antibiotics no better than placebo and linked to sepsis and colitis; discourages "chronic Lyme disease"; usually improves over time
- [Aucott et al., Int J Infect Dis 2022: Risk of post-treatment Lyme disease in ideally-treated early Lyme disease](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijid.2022.01.033) - 13.7% with prior Lyme disease met PTLD criteria vs 4.1% without
- [CDC (archived 2012): Post-treatment Lyme disease syndrome](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20121017192212/http://www.cdc.gov/lyme/postLDS/index.html) - earlier CDC estimate of about 10-20% with lingering symptoms after a recommended course
- [Klempner et al., NEJM 2001: Two controlled trials of antibiotic treatment for persistent symptoms](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM200107123450202) - IV then oral antibiotics no better than placebo
- [Berende et al., NEJM 2016: Randomized trial of longer-term therapy for symptoms attributed to Lyme disease (PLEASE)](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1505425) - 12 weeks of oral antibiotics after IV ceftriaxone did not improve quality of life vs placebo
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06611111: Ceftriaxone pulse dose for post-treatment Lyme disease](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06611111) - early Phase 1, 44 participants, SUNY Upstate, primary completion est. Dec 2026
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06915324: Transcranial direct current stimulation for post-treatment Lyme disease](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06915324) - Columbia University pilot for lasting cognitive symptoms, recruiting
- [Nadelman et al., NEJM 2001: Single-dose doxycycline after an Ixodes scapularis tick bite](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM200107123450201) - 200 mg within 72 hours; 87% efficacy (95% CI 25-98); 0.4% vs 3.2% developed erythema migrans
- [CDC: Caring for patients after a tick bite](https://www.cdc.gov/lyme/media/pdfs/Caring-for-Patients-after-a-Tick-Bite.pdf) - single-dose doxycycline when in a high-incidence area, attachment 36+ hours and within 72 hours of removal
- [Valneva/Pfizer: VALOR Phase 3 results (23 Mar 2026)](https://valneva.com/press-release/pfizer-and-valneva-announce-lyme-disease-vaccine-candidate-demonstrates-strong-efficacy-in-phase-3-valor-trial/) - 73.2% (95% CI 15.8-93.5) from 28 days after dose 4; 74.8% from 1 day after; ages 5+; well tolerated
- [pharmaphorum: Pfizer, Valneva shrug off Phase 3 Lyme vaccine miss (Mar 2026)](https://pharmaphorum.com/news/pfizer-valneva-shrug-phase-3-lyme-disease-vaccine-miss) - first pre-set criterion (CI lower bound above 20) missed after fewer cases than expected; second analysis met it; 2023 GCP problem cut enrollment
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05477524: VALOR Phase 3 of VLA15](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05477524) - 12,546 enrolled; completed Jan 2026
- [Valneva 6-K: EMA validates MAA for PF-07307405 (14 Aug 2026)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001836564/000162828026056999/finallymeematechnicalvalid.htm) - review started; doses at months 0, 2 and 5-9 plus a fourth a year later; no human Lyme vaccine approved
- [PharmaLive/BioSpace: Pfizer eyes Europe first for Lyme vaccine (4 Nov 2026)](https://www.pharmalive.com/pfizer-eyes-europe-first-for-lyme-vaccine-as-prevalence-rises-in-us/) - FDA submission discussions ongoing; CHMP verdict expected 2027; analysts suggest US filing by end of 2026
- [Steere et al., NEJM 1998: Vaccination against Lyme disease with recombinant OspA](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM199807233390401) - LYMErix trial: 49% efficacy after two doses, 76% after the third
- [Nigrovic and Thompson, Epidemiol Infect 2007: The Lyme vaccine, a cautionary tale](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0950268806007096) - FDA approved LYMErix in 1998; maker withdrew it about 3 years later amid side-effect fears and declining sales
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05975099: mRNA-1975 and mRNA-1982 Phase 1/2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05975099) - 807 enrolled; completed May 2025
- [IDWeek abstract P-1485 (Open Forum Infect Dis supplement): mRNA-1982 and mRNA-1975 interim Phase 1/2 results](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12792350/) - dose-dependent antibody rises with each injection
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07561294: mRNA-1982 Phase 2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07561294) - 366 enrolled, start Apr 2026, primary completion est. Nov 2028
- [PharmaVoice: Will Pfizer's Lyme disease gamble pay off?](https://www.pharmavoice.com/news/pfizer-valneva-lyme-disease-tick-vaccine/816308/) - Moderna said it would not invest in new late-stage vaccine trials
- [Tonix: Type C FDA meeting minutes on TNX-4800 Phase 2 (3 Aug 2026)](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/tonix-pharmaceuticals-announces-positive-minutes-from-type-c-fda-meeting-to-discuss-adaptive-phase-2-field-study-of-tnx-4800-a-human-anti-ospa-monoclonal-antibody-to-prevent-lyme-disease-in-the-u-s) - about 3,300 adults, 450 mg with a second dose 3 months later, start Q1 2027, most enrollment in 2028
- [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04863287: First study of 2217LS in healthy people](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04863287) - Phase 1 of the anti-OspA antibody (Lyme PrEP), completed 2022
- [UMass Chan: Preventative shot for Lyme disease enters clinical trial (Dec 2021)](https://www.umassmed.edu/news/articles/archives/2021/12/top-story-preventative-shot-for-lyme-disease-developed-at-umass-medical-school-enters-clinical-trial/) - Lyme PrEP antibody kills bacteria in the feeding tick, meant as a once-a-season shot

## Open questions

- Variants: split into early, late and post-treatment Lyme disease syndrome rather than one "late or persistent" group, because late arthritis and nerve disease usually respond to antibiotics (level 4) while PTLDS has no proven treatment (level 1).
- Late Lyme disease at level 4 is a judgement call near the 85% line: about 90% of adult Lyme arthritis resolves with antibiotics, but no comparable cure figure was found for late neurologic disease, which can leave lasting deficits.
- Prevention rung 1 rather than 0: single-dose doxycycline is a proven preventive drug (87% in one trial, wide confidence interval) but only applies to noticed, high-risk bites, so its population-level effect is much smaller. If PF-07307405 is licensed at about 73% efficacy it would move prevention to rung 2.
- PTLDS frequency varies with definition: CDC describes a 5-10% excess at 6 months over uninfected people, one prospective cohort found 13.7%, and older CDC material gave 10-20%.
- The late variant's "reached" year (1994) is approximate; it rests on the 2024 review's summary that oral antibiotics usually cure Lyme arthritis, not on a specific dated trial in the sources.
- The TNX-4800 Phase 2 readout date is not stated; with most enrollment in 2028 and a six-month endpoint, results look unlikely before 2029.
- Europe's burden: the 128,888 a year is reported cases only, from a Pfizer-funded review; true incidence is likely several times higher.
