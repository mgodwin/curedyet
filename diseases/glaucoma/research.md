# Glaucoma: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [Global prevalence of glaucoma and projections to 2040 (Ophthalmology, 2014)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24974815/) - 76 million in 2020
- [Global estimates of glaucoma blindness 2000-2020 (Eye, 2024)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41433-024-02995-5.pdf) - 3.6 million blind in 2020
- [ClinicalTrials.gov: NAMinG nicotinamide Phase 3](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05405868) - trial design and timing
