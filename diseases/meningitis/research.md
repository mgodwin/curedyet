# Meningitis: research

Evidence behind this disease's record. Every claim in disease.yaml should trace back to a source listed here.

## Sources

- [WHO fact sheet: Meningitis](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/meningitis) - 1 in 6 with bacterial meningitis die and 1 in 5 have lasting effects; causes by age; immediate antibiotics and dexamethasone; serogroup A conjugate vaccine cut disease 99%, no confirmed serogroup A case since 2017; Men5CV prequalified 2023, Niger's first Gavi-supported preventive campaign 2025
- [WHO: Defeating meningitis by 2030 roadmap](https://www.who.int/initiatives/defeating-meningitis-by-2030) - goals: eliminate bacterial meningitis epidemics, cut vaccine-preventable cases 50% and deaths 70%; WHA73.9 (2020); first WHO guidelines Apr 2025
- [GBD 2019 Meningitis Collaborators, Lancet Neurology 2023: Global burden of meningitis and its aetiologies, 1990-2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(23)00195-3) - 2.51 million cases and 236,000 deaths in 2019; 112,000 deaths under 5; pneumococcus 18.1% of deaths; Hib fell most in under-5s
- [van Ettekoven et al., JAMA Netw Open 2024: Global case fatality of bacterial meningitis over 80 years](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39093565/) - case fatality 18% overall, 32% before 1961, 15% after 2010; Listeria 27%, pneumococcus 24%, meningococcus 9%, H influenzae 11%
- [van de Beek et al., Nature Reviews Disease Primers 2016: Community-acquired bacterial meningitis](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrdp.2016.74) - pneumococcal meningitis case fatality 20-37% in high-income countries, up to 51% in low-income
- [CDC: Bacterial meningitis](https://www.cdc.gov/meningitis/about/bacterial-meningitis.html) - leading US causes, antibiotics as soon as possible, vaccines for Hib, meningococcus, pneumococcus; GBS screening in pregnancy; prophylaxis for contacts
- [CDC: Viral meningitis](https://www.cdc.gov/meningitis/about/viral-meningitis.html) - most common type; non-polio enteroviruses most common cause; most recover in 7-10 days; no specific treatment in most cases; vaccines for measles, mumps, chickenpox prevent some causes
- [Contagion Live: WHO global bacterial meningitis guidelines (May 2025)](https://www.contagionlive.com/view/with-its-global-bacterial-meningitis-guidelines-who-aims-to-speed-up-diagnosis-treatment) - first WHO guidelines Apr 2025: immediate IV ceftriaxone/cefotaxime, corticosteroids with first antibiotic dose
- [Piret and Boivin, Clin Microbiol Rev 2020: Immunomodulatory strategies in herpes simplex virus encephalitis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7018500) - untreated HSV encephalitis mortality 70%; IV acyclovir with modern diagnosis close to 20%
- [Gavi: Meningitis A nearly eliminated in Africa through vaccination](https://www.gavi.org/news/media-room/meningitis-nearly-eliminated-africa-through-vaccination) - MenAfriVac introduced 2010; more than 235 million vaccinated by 2016; serogroup A disappeared where used
- [WHO: In world first, Nigeria introduces new 5-in-1 vaccine against meningitis (Apr 2024)](https://www.who.int/news/item/12-04-2024-in-world-first--nigeria-introduces-new-5-in-1-vaccine-against-meningitis) - Men5CV (MenFive) covers A, C, W, Y, X; prequalified Jul 2023; Nigeria campaign Mar 2024
- [Healio: FDA approves GSK's pentavalent meningococcal vaccine (Feb 2025)](https://cm.healio.com/news/pediatrics/20250217/fda-approves-gsks-pentavalent-meningococcal-vaccine) - Penmenvy (MenABCWY) approved for ages 10-25, after Pfizer's Penbraya
- [Healio: 4CMenB demonstrates effectiveness, does not induce herd protection (2020)](https://www.healio.com/news/pediatrics/20200214/4cmenb-demonstrates-effectiveness-does-not-induce-herd-protection) - England infant MenB programme (Ladhani et al., NEJM 2020): about 59% effectiveness with booster, 277 cases prevented in three years
- [CDC: Pneumococcal vaccines](https://www.cdc.gov/pneumococcal/vaccines/index.html) - PCV15, PCV20, PCV21; recommended for children under 5 and adults 50+; cannot prevent all cases
- [Vaxcyte 8-K, 4 Nov 2026 (Q3 results)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001649094/000164909426000051/pcvx-20261104xexx991.htm) - VAX-31 OPUS-1 adult Phase 3 positive Oct 2026; OPUS-2/3 and infant Phase 2 data 1H 2027; BLA planned 1H 2028
- [BEATRIX trial listing (GBS6, NCT07160244)](https://ctv.veeva.com/study/beatrix-a-study-to-learn-about-a-group-b-streptococcus-vaccine-in-healthy-pregnant-women-and-their) - Phase 3, about 6,000 pregnant participants, start Aug 2025, estimated completion Mar 2029
- [Jarvis et al., NEJM 2022: Single-dose liposomal amphotericin B treatment for cryptococcal meningitis (AMBITION-cm)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2111904) - 10-week mortality 24.8% vs 28.7%; five African countries; fewer side effects
- [WHO: Guidelines for diagnosing, preventing and managing cryptococcal disease among adults, adolescents and children living with HIV (2022)](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240052178) - single high-dose liposomal amphotericin B with flucytosine and fluconazole preferred; CrAg screening and pre-emptive fluconazole; access gaps
- [Rajasingham et al., Lancet Infect Dis 2022: Global burden of HIV-associated cryptococcal infection in 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9701154) - 152,000 cryptococcal meningitis cases and 112,000 deaths (19% of AIDS-related deaths); down from 181,100 deaths in 2014
- [Awotiwon et al., Cochrane 2018: Primary antifungal prophylaxis for cryptococcal disease in HIV-positive people](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30156270/) - prophylaxis cut cryptococcal disease by 71% (RR 0.29) but not clearly all-cause mortality
- [EnACT3 trial listing (oral amphotericin MAT2203, NCT05541107)](https://ctv.veeva.com/study/encochleated-oral-amphotericin-for-cryptococcal-meningitis-trial3) - Phase 3 listed as not yet enrolling; Phase 2 EnACT 18-week survival 85-90%
- [WHO Macao/China: China produces world's first vaccine against virus that causes hand, foot and mouth disease (Dec 2015)](https://www.who.int/macaochina/news/detail/08-12-2015-china-produces-world-s-first-vaccine-against-virus-that-causes-hand-foot-and-mouth-disease) - first EV71 vaccine licensed in China, about 97% effective against EV71 disease
- [Zhu et al., NEJM 2014: Efficacy, safety and immunogenicity of an EV71 vaccine in China](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4008769) - 94.8% efficacy against EV71 disease

## Research log

- 2026-11-05: Baseline research for the first Cure Watch record. Split into bacterial, viral and cryptococcal (fungal) variants because outlooks, treatment and prevention differ sharply. Rated the disease at 3, anchored on bacterial meningitis.

## Open questions

- Bacterial level 3 counts lasting effects as not fully cured. On survival alone, rich-country outcomes for meningococcal and Hib meningitis (about 9-11% case fatality) would clear 85%, while pneumococcal and Listeria would not; the pooled WHO figures mix rich and poor countries.
- Bacterial prevention rated 2 (partial) for the typical person: conjugate vaccines are strong against covered strains, but GBS, Listeria, E. coli and non-vaccine pneumococcal serotypes remain. A case could be made for 3 for meningococcal or Hib disease taken alone.
- Viral variant rated 1 for consistency with other self-limiting viral infections (common cold, measles, RSV), although nearly everyone recovers fully.
- Fungal survival figure is from African trials; no good rich-country outcome figure was found. Matinas's EnACT3 Phase 3 status is unclear (registry last verified 2022).
- The WHO fact sheet states Niger ran the first Gavi-supported preventive Men5CV campaign in 2025; no independent confirmation found.
- No dated readout found for adjunct therapies in bacterial meningitis (complement inhibitors, daptomycin remain animal or small single-arm studies).
